### Changed
- `ufispace/s9600-30dx` (4.1.1 to 4.1.2), `ufispace/s9600-32x` (3.1.1 to 3.1.2), `ufispace/s9610-46dx` (4.1.1 to 4.1.2) and `ufispace/s9720-56ed` (3.1.2 to 3.1.3): the build whose hardware installation guide prints a maximum system power other than the device's states its own `power-max-w`. The `dc` builds of the 30DX, 32X and 46DX state 1276 W, 609 W and 1524 W (the device keeps its AC build's 1300 W, 621.3 W and 1534 W), and the `ac` build of the 56ED states 2280 W (the device keeps its DC build's 2339 W). Each device's power note says so. The configuration texts are unchanged and now agree with what they export.
- The test over configuration descriptions no longer lists any contradiction, and fails if one comes back.
- The NetBox and Nautobot device types of these four devices, and of the same hardware under three NOS vendors (20 files), change in the drawing version their `comments` quote. The default `ac` build of the 56ED also now exports 2280 W where it exported 2339 W; a DCIM that already imported that type keeps the old figure until it imports it again. No model, part number, slug, port or bay name moves. This order closes no gap row.
