### Added
- Cisco Catalyst 2960-S, second batch (Refs #1275): four 1RU stackable switches with a built-in AC supply and two
  SFP+ uplinks, each with a USB Type A port, USB mini-Type B and RJ-45 consoles, an Ethernet management port, a
  FlexStack module slot and an RPS connector. The WS-C2960S-48LPD-L (`cisco/ws-c2960s-48lpd`, 48 10/100/1000 PoE+
  ports, 370 W), the WS-C2960S-48TD-L (`cisco/ws-c2960s-48td`, 48 ports, no PoE), the WS-C2960S-24PD-L
  (`cisco/ws-c2960s-24pd`, 24 PoE+ ports, 370 W) and the WS-C2960S-24TD-L (`cisco/ws-c2960s-24td`, 24 ports, no
  PoE). The FlexStack slot seats the blank cover and accepts the C2960S-STACK module (an example configuration on
  each); the RPS receptacle is the part `cisco/rps2300-connector@2`, so each rear has a connector with a mate. Each
  device has three configurations, the `ac` base build, one orderable named for its only SKU and the module example,
  twelve in all. The DCIM exports gain one device type per tool for each of the four SKUs.

### Changed
- The fronts of the four 2960-S second-batch devices are drawn in Cisco's dark teal edge, charcoal panel and white
  console surround, which the four model stencils agree on, with the jack-block rings and plates, number tags, tick
  bands, SFP+ frames and numeral tabs, console and management plates and arrow lamps of the WS-C2960S-48FPD-L. The
  48LPD-L and 24TD-L were drawn in the default grey housing before. The rears are charcoal with the warning label
  over the RPS plate and the fan web and cut edges as photographed; the rating figures stay blank because the held
  photograph prints figures that match no row of the guide's tables.
