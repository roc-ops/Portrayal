### Changed
- The DC rear of the Juniper EX2200-24T and of the EX2300-24T now places the terminal block as a part instead of drawing it as art: `juniper/ex2200-dc-terminal@1` (terminals B-, A-, B+ and A+) and `juniper/ex2300-dc-terminal@1` (INPUT 1+, INPUT 1-, INPUT 2+ and INPUT 2-), two parts because the two blocks differ in size and pitch. Each composes four terminal screws, every one a seat a ring lug lands on, and states one DC input port per feed, two poles each, with the roles and the one wire size the vendor's hardware guide gives (14 AWG), and no voltage because the guide prints a range. The EX2200's block states the guide's 3.5 A and its two feeds' sides; the EX2300's states no current, because its guide and its rear legend disagree (recorded as a gap). The EX2200's block size and pole pitch are estimated, since the guide's figure has no scale and no photograph is held; the EX2300's are measured off its rear photograph. The art the two devices drew in its place is gone.
- The EX2300's `dc` configuration now states that it is powered by DC; it said nothing, so the switch's AC default applied to it.
- The two switches take a minor each, with their locks and the drawing version line of their exported device types (12 files in NetBox and Nautobot). The exporter lists the two new parts as power inputs, so the DC device types (EX2200-24T-4G-DC and EX2300-24T-DC) now export a `dc-terminal` power port, as the AC types export `ac-inlet`; no existing port, interface or id moves. Two rows of the records file are gone, and the two switches' decor warnings (lint L197) with them.
