### Added
- Raritan PXO-2401CR and PXO-2402R compact PDUs, 220 x 44 x 133 mm, each with six views: four outlets, a C20 inlet and a
  controller face with two Ethernet jacks, a console jack, a sensor jack, USB-B and USB-A jacks, a reset button, a display and
  four buttons. The PXO-2401CR's four C13 outlets are locking, each with its release button. Positions on the outlet face are read
  off a photograph and are estimates. This closes no row of the port records.
- Parts for the PXO: `raritan/pxo-usb-b@1` (a USB-B jack, which presents the new `usb-b` interface) and `generic/usb-b-plug@1`
  (the plug that mates it), `raritan/pxo-lock-button@1`, `raritan/pxo-lcd@1` and `raritan/pxo-button@1`. The lock button, the plug and
  their sizes are estimates, declared in each contract. The maker is registered as `raritan`.
