### Changed
- The Digi Connect EZ 16 and 32 and the Connect IT 16 and 48 now draw the fan grilles as the maker's photographs show them.
  Each grille is a disc with hexagonal openings, where it was drawn as two dashed rings. This reaches the three power supply
  and fan parts these devices seat, each of which takes a patch version.
  The supply case screws are drawn at the counts the photograph shows: six on a Connect IT supply and four on a Connect EZ supply.
  The Connect IT front also draws the bright metal frame that joins each block of sixteen serial jacks.
  The Connect IT rear draws its system fan as a honeycomb. Every position and pitch is read from an 800 px photograph
  and is an estimate. Each of the four devices takes a patch version. This closes no row of the conformance records.
