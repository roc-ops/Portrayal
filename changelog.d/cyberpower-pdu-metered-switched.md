### Added
- CyberPower PDU15M2F12R and PDU41021 rack PDUs, each with six views. The PDU15M2F12R is a 1U metered PDU, 445 x 44 x 57 mm: a breaker button, a
  two-digit red amp meter and two NEMA 5-15R outlets on the front, twelve more 5-15R outlets, an external site ground and a fixed 15 ft cord with a
  NEMA 5-15P plug on the rear. The PDU41021 is a 1U switched PDU, 433 x 44 x 215 mm: eight NEMA 5-15R outlets with a lamp each, a fixed 12 ft cord
  with a NEMA 5-15P plug, a display, Select and Enter buttons, an Ethernet jack, three further RJ45 jacks and a USB jack on the front. Its eight rear
  outlets are stated by the data sheet and shown by no document, so its rear is left unshown and not drawn. The PDU15M2F12R's twelve rear outlets are
  placed by estimate, evenly across the span of the eight its manual draws. Every front position is read off an angled photograph and is an estimate;
  the PDU15M2F12R's rear is read off a line drawing. This closes no row of the port records.
