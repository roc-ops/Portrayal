### Added
- **NVIDIA ConnectX-7 for OCP NIC 3.0: two cards**, from the ConnectX-7 Cards for
  OCP Spec 3.0 User Manual, each on its thumbscrew (pull tab) bracket and each one
  module for the two part numbers that share its board (they differ in crypto only).
  `nvidia/mcx75343-ocp3-tsff` is the tall small form factor card with one OSFP
  cage (MCX75343AMC-NEAC and MCX75343AMS-NEAC); `nvidia/mcx753436-ocp3-sff` is the
  small form factor card with two QSFP112 cages (MCX753436MC-HEAB and
  MCX753436MS-HEAB). Each states its form-factor key, the bracket's 82.50 width
  and its height (17.80 and 15.1) from the bracket drawing, the board's 115.00
  depth, the typical power the manual prints, and a lamp pair per port. Vents,
  windows and lamps are measured off the figure; the grey is a reading of a
  rendering, as no photograph of either card is held. Neither is seated in any
  device: the Dell R660's OCP bay names Dell cards only. The change closes no
  conformance record.
