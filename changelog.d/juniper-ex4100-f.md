### Added
- Juniper EX4100-F-24T, EX4100-F-24P, EX4100-F-48T and EX4100-F-48P (#39): fixed 1RU Gigabit access switches with four
  Virtual Chassis and four uplink SFP+ ports, from the EX4100-F Switch Hardware Guide (PDF created 2026-10-01). Each
  model is one device with one configuration, its own part number, and each exports as a DCIM device type. The power
  supply and fans are built into the chassis, so no bay is modelled. The fanless EX4100-F-12T and -12P are not
  modelled yet.
