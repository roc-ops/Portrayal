  ports, four USB 2.0 ports, two SIM slots and three antenna jacks. The ACM7004-2-L adds two RJ45 network ports and a
  digital I/O header. The ACM7004-5-L adds a combo uplink and a four-port switch. Size and power are the data sheet's.
  Positions are read off the maker's front and rear photographs, so they are estimates with their method written in each
  device. Three new connectors are registered, each with a part and a mate. They are the mini SIM slot, the
  four-position DC housing and the eight-position digital I/O header. The other ACM7000 models, the OM2200, the OM1200
  and the CM8100 DC and cellular models are not included. No existing device, part, port or export changes. This closes
