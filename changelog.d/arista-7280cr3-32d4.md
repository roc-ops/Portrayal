### Added
- The Arista 7280CR3-32D4 (`arista/7280cr3-32d4`, #1204): a 1RU switch router with 32 100G QSFP ports and four
  400G QSFP-DD ports, its front panel with the port lamps and system lamps, and its rear with the management,
  console and USB ports, two power supply bays and three fan bays. The 7280CR3K-32D4 and 7280CR3K-32D4A builds
  share the panel and are orderable configurations, each front-to-rear and rear-to-front: DCS-7280CR3-32D4-F and -R,
  DCS-7280CR3K-32D4-F and -R, and DCS-7280CR3K-32D4A-F and -R. Two DC builds are examples with no part number.
- The 7280CR3-32D4's field-replaceable modules, family-prefixed: the 1000 W AC and DC power supplies and the 1RU fan
  module, each in its red front-to-rear and blue rear-to-front form (`arista/7280cr3-pwr-1000-ac`, `-ac-rev`,
  `-dc`, `-dc-rev`, `arista/7280cr3-fan` and `-fan-rev`).
- The DCIM device types for the six orderable builds, in the NetBox and Nautobot exports, and the module types for
  PWR-1011-AC-RED, -AC-BLUE, -DC-RED, -DC-BLUE, FAN-7011H-F and FAN-7011H-R.
