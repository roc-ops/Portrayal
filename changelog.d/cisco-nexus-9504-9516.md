### Added
- Cisco Nexus 9500 chassis, batch 2: the Nexus 9504 (`cisco/n9k-c9504`, N9K-C9504, 7 RU) and the Nexus 9516
  (`cisco/n9k-c9516`, N9K-C9516, 21 RU). The 9504 has four line-card slots (each holding its blank until the
  line cards are added), two supervisor slots and four power-supply slots lying flat either side of the chassis
  lamps on the front, and three fan-tray, six fabric-module and two system-controller slots on the rear. The
  9516 has sixteen line-card slots, two supervisor slots and ten power-supply slots on the front, and the same
  rear. Both take the supervisors, system controller, power supplies and blanks added with the Nexus 9508. The
  line cards and the rack kits follow.
- Cisco Nexus 9504 fan trays `cisco/n9k-c9504-fan` and `cisco/n9k-c9504-fan2`; fabric modules
  `cisco/n9k-c9504-fm`, `-fm-s`, `-fm-e`, `-fm-g` and `-fm-r`; and the blanks `cisco/n9k-c9504-fm-cv` and
  `cisco/n9k-c9504-fan-pwr`.
- Cisco Nexus 9516 fan tray `cisco/n9k-c9516-fan`; fabric modules `cisco/n9k-c9516-fm`, `-fm-e` and `-fm-e2`;
  and the blanks `cisco/n9k-c9516-fm-cv` and `cisco/n9k-c9516-fm-z`.
