### Added
- The Eaton Tripp Lite series B097-016, a 16-port serial console server for a desktop or a 19 inch
  rack (`eaton/b097-016` 0.1.0). The Rear carries the 16 RJ45 serial ports in two rows of eight with
  the Cisco straight pin-out, the two RJ45 Ethernet ports NET1 and NET2 and the two IEC inlets of its
  two built-in supplies; the Front carries the RJ45 console port, two USB-A ports and four status
  lamps. It is drawn from existing parts, and every position is estimated because the maker publishes
  only two small photographs of the faces. Where the maker's sources disagree (the width of the body,
  the weight, the number of USB ports, the type of the console port) the device says so in its gaps and
  states neither weight. One device adds one version record and two DCIM device-type files; no existing
  device or part changes and no record of the conformance list closes.
