### Added
- Four Moxa CN2600 16-port terminal servers as four devices: `moxa/cn2610-16-2ac`, `moxa/cn2650-16-2ac`, `moxa/cn2610-16` and `moxa/cn2650-16`.
  Each has all six faces. The front has the LCD panel, four push buttons, the Reset button and two rows of 16 Tx and Rx lamps.
  It also has the Ready lamp, LAN1 and LAN2 lamps and PWR1 and PWR2 lamps, drawn off.
  The rear has a ground screw, a console jack, two LAN jacks and 16 RJ45 serial ports in two groups of eight.
  The two -2AC models have two AC inlets with a rocker switch each, and the other two have one.
  The CN2610 models are RS-232 and the CN2650 models are RS-232/422/485.
  The sides, the underside and the rack ears are read from the maker's 2D CAD sheet and declared estimates.
  Each side draws the plate at its front end that the sheet shows, with its six holes.
  No part is added. The devices place the LCD panel, push buttons and power rocker of the NPort 5650-16.
  They also place the library's RJ45, AC inlet and ground parts.
  The eight-port, DB9, wide-temperature and high-voltage CN2600 models are not modelled.
  This closes no rows of the port records.
