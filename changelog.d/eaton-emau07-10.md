### Added
- The Eaton ePDU G3 managed rack PDU EMAU07-10: a 2U horizontal unit with 16 IEC C13 outlets in two sections of eight.
  It has two 20 A two-pole breakers, a fixed 10 ft cord ending in a NEMA L6-30P and the network module.
  It is drawn on its front, rear and top from the maker's technical drawing, and its ends and underside are built plain (Refs #1472).
- The part `eaton/g3-cord-l6-30p-front`, the fixed cord of that unit, which leaves the front face through a round gland.
  It exports as a `nema-l6-30p` power port, as the cord of the EMIT04-10 does.
