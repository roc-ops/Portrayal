### Added
- The Vertiv PowerIT VP5N31A1, a 1U switched rack PDU with twelve combination C13/C19 outlets on the rear in two
  groups of six, a fixed input cord with an L6-30P plug, two breakers, twelve outlet lamps and an IMD-5M monitoring
  module on the front, drawn from the maker's photographs and sized from its specification table. The combination
  outlet is a new part with its own connector key that a C14 or a C20 plug mates; it exports as an `other` power
  outlet labelled Vertiv combination C13/C19. The change closes no rows of the port-usable records.
