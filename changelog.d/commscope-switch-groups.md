### Changed
- The ports on the three switches of the CommScope CH3000 chassis (the AB32S1G and AB32S1T A/B alternate
  routing switches and the OS32M2B dual 2x1 optical switch) now join port groups, so a port on a switch
  seated in the chassis says what it is for. The three F-type jacks of each A/B switch are one `traffic`
  group, and the six SC/APC ports of the optical switch another. The data sheets print no data rate for
  either, so no group states a speed. Part ids, positions and drawn geometry are unchanged. Each contract
  takes a minor and the CH3000 chassis, which seats them, a patch. It closes no conformance record.
