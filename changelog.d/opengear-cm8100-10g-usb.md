### Added
- Opengear CM8116-10G-USB and CM8148-10G-USB console managers: new 1RU devices with 16 or 48 RJ45 serial ports,
  two 10G SFP+ cages, two 1G RJ45 ports, a front RJ45 console and two USB 3.0 ports, and on the rear eight USB-C serial
  ports and two IEC C14 inlets. Front and rear are drawn from the maker's photographs, so every position is an estimate
  with its method written in the device. The serial lines are console server ports, so the NetBox and Nautobot device
  types list them under `console-server-ports`. The data sheet gives no envelope or weight for the -USB models, so
  the base model's 440 x 265 x 44 mm is used and declared borrowed; the rack kit is left out for want of a drawing.
  No existing device, part, port or export changes. This closes no row of the conformance records.
