### Added
- Juniper EX2200-48T, EX2200-48P, EX2200-24T, EX2200-24P, EX2200-C-12T and EX2200-C-12P (#39): the end-of-life EX2200
  line the hardware guide (2022-02-02) shows, one device per front panel. The EX2200 models are 1RU Gigabit access
  switches of 24 or 48 ports with four SFP uplinks, with and without PoE, and a rear that exposes a third exhaust opening
  on the PoE models; the EX2200-24T-4G-DC is the 24T's `dc` configuration, a four-terminal block in place of the AC inlet.
  The EX2200-C models are fanless 269 mm boxes of 12 ports with two dual-purpose RJ-45/SFP uplinks, a mini-USB console and
  a heatsink on the PoE+ model. Each part number is a configuration exporting as a DCIM device type. The power supply and
  fans are built into every chassis, so no bay is modelled, and no component is added.
