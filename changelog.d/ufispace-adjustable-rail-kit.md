### Added
- `ufispace/adjustable-rail-771-kit@1`, the adjustable mounting rail kit of the UfiSpace deep
  routers and switches, and its part `ufispace/adjustable-rail-771@1`. It is a sliding kit of one
  four-post configuration, with a rack depth of 558.8 to 838.2 mm (the guides print 22 to 33
  inches, converted at 25.4 mm to the inch) and a `travel` of `full`, because the guides say only
  that the middle rail is pulled out fully extended. The guides give one size for the whole rail,
  771 x 44 x 22 mm, and none for its inner, middle or outer member, so the rail is one part and
  the kit's `gaps` say no member is dimensioned.

### Changed
- Seventeen UfiSpace devices list the kit as shipped in the box: the S9710-76D, S9700-23D,
  S9700-53DX, S9705-48D, S9720-56ED, S9725-64E, S9600-28DX, S9600-30DX, S9600-32X, S9600-56DX,
  S9600-64X, S9610-36D, S9610-46DX, S9610-48DX, S9611-36D, S9321-64E and S9321-64EO. Each takes a
  patch version and its lock, and the comments of its exported device types gain one "Rail kits"
  sentence. They state no `chassis.ears`, and no face, port or placement changes. Nothing a rack
  file or a page names is removed or renamed. This closes no conformance rows.
- The rack mounting design note's UfiSpace rows now match the guides: seventeen models print the
  771 mm rail, and the other deep models print a shorter one (626, 622, 598.3 or 568 mm), which
  this kit does not serve.
