### Added
- Juniper EX2500 10 GbE top-of-rack switch, from the EX2500 Ethernet Switch Hardware Guide (read via a
  mirror copy) and the EX2500 datasheet: `juniper/ex2500-24f`, one device with the two models as
  configurations, `fb` (EX2500-24F-FB, front-to-back airflow) and `bf` (EX2500-24F-BF, back-to-front).
  The front has 24 SFP+ ports with a speed lamp and a link lamp each, two management jacks, a mini-USB
  console, a reset button and four system lamps; the rear has two AC inlets, two screws and the protective-earth symbol at its left end, placed from
  the guide's rear figure, which draws no lug or screw at it. The supplies and fans are
  built in, so there are no bays. The rack mounting kits are not modelled.
