### Changed
- The Cisco Catalyst 2960S-24PD-L, 2960S-48FPD-L and 2960S-48LPD-L (`cisco/ws-c2960s-24pd`, `cisco/ws-c2960s-48fpd` and `cisco/ws-c2960s-48lpd`, Refs #1275) draw their right wall.
  It has 34 openings: 25 peanut slots, 5 small round holes and 4 small rectangles, and a light metal tab in the last rectangle.
- The two ends of the wall are read from Figure 1-12 of the Catalyst 2960-S Switch Hardware Installation Guide.
  The figure is an isometric drawing of a PoE model.
  Each place is mapped to millimetres by the wall's 386 mm length and 44.45 mm height.
  A place along the wall is good to about 8 mm.
- The rack bracket in the figure covers the wall from 92 to 229 mm.
  A round hole and two slots there are the pattern photographed on the 299 mm models.
  A gap on each device says so.
- The openings stay drawn as marks in a stand-in dark, and the wall keeps the device's colour.
  The lit rim of each opening is left to a later touch.
- Each device takes a patch bump (1.0.3 to 1.0.4). No port, lamp or group id moves.
