### Added
- The Vertiv Avocent ACS800 compact serial console as one device, the 4-port unit with an analog modem
  (`vertiv/acs804meac-400`), the one model its data sheet orders. It draws both faces: the MODEM, DIGITAL IN and
  SENSOR jacks, the DIGITAL OUT connector, four USB ports and two lamps on the front, and the 12VDC jack, console
  jack, two LAN jacks and four serial ports on the rear. No drawing is held, so every position is measured off the
  maker's photographs and is declared estimated; the rear of the four-port unit is not photographed and takes the
  first four jacks of the data sheet's eight-port picture.
- Two parts and one connector key: `vertiv/acs800-digital-out@1` presents the new `screw-terminal-4-relay` key for
  the four-pin DIGITAL OUT connector, and `vertiv/acs800-digital-out-plug@1`, a declared stand-in for the cable
  connector, mates it.
