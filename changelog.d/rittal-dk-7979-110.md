### Added
- **Rittal DK 7979.110** (`rittal/dk-7979-110`): a single-phase basic PDU, 0U, 695 mm
  long, with a fixed 3 m cord ending in a 16 A CEE plug of three wires, twelve IEC C13
  outlets in two blocks of six and one IEC C19 outlet. All six faces are stated; the
  two push-button attachments on the back are placed 622.3 mm apart as the maker's
  drawing dimensions them. The cord is a part of its own,
  `rittal/pdu-cord-cee-16a-1p@1`, beside the three-phase cord: the plug and the number
  of wires differ. It exports as a power port of type `other` with a label, as the
  three-phase cord does; the exporter's two tables gain that one entry each. The C13
  and C19 outlets and the button are the parts the other DK 7979 devices already use.
  The device closes no row of the records file.
