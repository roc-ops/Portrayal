### Added
- An `outlet-run` segment can take a `pattern` in place of a `ref`: the outlet
  kinds in the order they repeat, each with the shift of its own seat from the
  pitch. A bank can take an `id-format` of its own, for a strip whose sections
  are lettered. The Eaton EVMI2130X, whose C13 and C39 outlets alternate in six
  sections of seven, is authored this way. It expands to the same outlets, so
  its version and its exports do not move. The change closes no conformance
  rows.
