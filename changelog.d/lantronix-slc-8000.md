### Added
- `lantronix/slc-8000`, the Lantronix SLC 8000 console manager as a base chassis: six faces, a 1U body of 438.15 by
  44.45 by 304.8 mm, an LCD, a keypad, a console jack, two USB host ports and a modem jack on the front, and two
  network jacks, an AC inlet and three declared I/O module bays on the rear. The bays present the new interface
  `lantronix-slc-io` and hold no part yet, because no document held gives an I/O module its size. The memory card
  slot the front shows is left as a gap until the library has a card interface. Positions are read off the maker's
  photographs and are estimates.
- `lantronix/slc-lcd@1` and `lantronix/slc-keypad@1`, the two front-panel parts of that chassis. Their sizes are
  estimates, and each contract says so.
