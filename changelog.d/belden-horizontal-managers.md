### Added
- The Belden horizontal cable managers, eight devices under the new `belden` vendor: `bhh191ur`,
  `bhh192ur`, `bhh193ur` and `bhh194ur` (1U to 4U, molded fingers and a hinging cover),
  `9512-1901` (1U finger stock with a cover), `9510-1901` (1U, six saddle rings) and `9511-1903`
  and `9511-1904` (3U and 4U ring managers, eight saddle rings by the data sheet thumbnails)
  (#1103, part of #1091). Each is a `rack-face` sheet part 482.6 mm wide, sized from Belden's
  technical data sheets (revision 05-31-2024). The data sheets carry no drawing, so the finger
  pitch, the ring size and spacing and the cover's shape are read off their thumbnails and are
  `estimated`. The finger parts state a `duct` guide, each ring a `ring` guide, and each cover is
  its own removable part with a `cover-off` configuration; a cable is laid along a duct and
  through a ring in the rack kit. The 9511 data sheets' two heights are settled from the rack
  units (5.25 in and 7.0 in) and recorded as gaps. The data sheets give no cable capacity or bend
  radius, so none is recorded. The lacing bar `9512-1903` waits for tie points on rings and ducts
  (#1009).
