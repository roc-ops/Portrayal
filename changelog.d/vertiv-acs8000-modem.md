### Added
- Four more devices for the Vertiv Avocent ACS8000 advanced console server. They are the dual-AC units with a built-in
  analog modem and 8, 16, 32 or 48 RJ45 serial ports (`vertiv/acs8008mdac-400`, `vertiv/acs8016mdac-400`,
  `vertiv/acs8032mdac-400` and `vertiv/acs8048mdac-400`). Each is the dual-AC front with one RJ45 modem jack at the
  right end. The jack is read off the maker's installation guide figure, as no photograph of a modem unit is held.
  The rear of each device is the rear of the unit with the same port count. No part is added and no existing part or
  device changes. No row of the port records is closed.
