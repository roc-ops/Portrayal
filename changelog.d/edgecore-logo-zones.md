### Changed
- Thirteen Edgecore fronts reserve the place of the maker's printed logotype as a
  `logo-zone` region at its measured position and size: the AGR560, AGR400
  (AS7946-30XB), AGR420 (AS7946-74XKSB), AIS800-64D, AIS800-64O, COR550, COR580,
  CSR200, CSR430, CSR440, EPS112, EXP100-32X and EXP400-32X. Nothing is drawn
  there and no drawing moves; the plain `Edge-core` text on the CSR200, CSR430 and
  CSR440 stays. Each device takes a patch version, and the `Drawing version` line
  of its NetBox and Nautobot exports moves with it.
- Three fronts that print the mark are left out for now: the CSR180 and EXP800-16O
  pictures are taken from above, so their two axes do not agree well enough to
  measure from, and the CSR310 is being edited in another open change.
