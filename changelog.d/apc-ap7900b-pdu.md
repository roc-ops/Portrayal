### Added
- The APC by Schneider Electric NetShelter AP7900B switched 1U rack PDU, with eight NEMA 5-15R outlets and a fixed input
  cord with a NEMA 5-15P plug, drawn on all six faces from the maker's product data sheet. The data sheet holds only small
  photographs, so every position on the outlet face is an estimate and the device says so. One new part of the maker:
  `apc/pdu-cord-5-15p@1`, the cord's strain relief. The DCIM export gains a power-port type for it, `nema-5-15p`, as the
  other fixed cords carry. The device and the part are new, so nothing that exists changes; two rows of DCIM device types
  are new (NetBox and Nautobot). No record row is closed.
