### Added
- ZPE Systems joins the makers, with the Nodegrid Serial Console S, 48 ports (`zpe/nsc-t48s`
  0.1.0), modelled as its dual-AC build with two SFP cages. The Front carries the HDMI port, one
  USB 2.0 port, the PWR and SYS lamps, the reset button, two fan modules and twelve USB 2.0 ports in
  six stacked pairs; the Rear carries the 48 RJ45 serial ports in three blocks of eight columns, the
  two SFP cages, the console RJ45, the USB 3.0 port and two AC inlets with their rocker switches.
  Four new parts (`zpe/nsc-fan-module`, `zpe/nsc-power-rocker`, `zpe/nsc-hdmi` and the fixed rail kit
  `zpe/rail-180-395` with its four pieces) are drawn from one small photograph or a sketch with no
  section, and each says so as an estimate. The device is listed with the rail kit. A new device is
  a minor bump for the lock and adds two DCIM device-type files; no existing device or part changes.
