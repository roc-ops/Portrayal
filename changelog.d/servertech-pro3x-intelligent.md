  Each has 18 HDOT C13 and 18 HDOT Cx outlets in six groups of six behind two two-pole breakers.

  sensor jack, aux jack, USB-A port and USB-B port are modelled parts.
  The panel's size is estimated, as the sheet gives no scale. The change closes no rows of the port-usable records.
