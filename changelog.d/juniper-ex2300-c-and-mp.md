### Added
- Juniper EX2300-C-12P, EX2300-C-12T, EX2300-24MP and EX2300-48MP (#39): the rest of the EX2300 line the hardware
  guide (2025-08-12) shows, one device per front panel. The compact EX2300-C models are fanless 279 mm boxes with 12
  Gigabit ports and two SFP+/SFP uplinks; their -TAA and -VC part numbers are configurations, each exporting as a DCIM
  device type. The EX2300-24MP and EX2300-48MP are 1RU Multigigabit switches (eight and sixteen 2.5G ports) with four and
  six uplinks and side-to-side cooling; the bare part number is their one configuration. The power supply and fans are
  built into every chassis, so no bay is modelled, and no component is added.
