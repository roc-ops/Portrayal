### Added
- The Server Technology PRO3X Basic 3A06, a 0U rack PDU with 36 outlets (18 HDOT C13 and 18 HDOT Cx) in six
  groups of six, each behind its own two-pole breaker, with no controller, no metering and no switching, and the
  back's mounting positions, drawn from the maker's outline drawing and wiring diagram. Two new parts: its breaker
  window and its fixed cord, which exports as an `other` power port labelled as an IEC 60309 3P+G 9h plug. The change
  closes no rows of the port-usable records.
