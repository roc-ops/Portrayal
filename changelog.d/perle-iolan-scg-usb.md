### Added
- Six Perle IOLAN SCG console servers with USB or mixed rear modules: `perle/iolan-scg18-u`, `perle/iolan-scg34-u`,
  `perle/iolan-scg34-ru`, `perle/iolan-scg50-u`, `perle/iolan-scg50-rru` and `perle/iolan-scg50-ruu`. Each is the chassis
  of its size with its rear bays filled as the maker's photographs show them.
- A USB module is 16 USB 3.0 Type-A ports in two staggered bands of four columns. The positions are read off the
  installation guide's photograph and are good to about 1.5 mm.
- The mixed forms put an RJ45 module in bay 1 and a USB module in bay 2. The 200 pixel product photographs show this
  order, which the model names alone would not give.
- The fronts are those of the R models. The rear thumbscrews of each fitted module are drawn as estimates.
- Not drawn yet: the printed marks and labels, the DC builds with their terminal blocks, the S forms and the L forms.
