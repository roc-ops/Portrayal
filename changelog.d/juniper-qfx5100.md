### Added
- Juniper `juniper/qfx5100-48s` (QFX5100-48S, 48 SFP+ and 6 QSFP+ ports) and
  `juniper/qfx5100-48t` (QFX5100-48T, 48 10GBASE-T and 6 QSFP+ ports), each 1RU
  at `modelled` maturity. Each is one front panel with orderable configurations
  for the AC and DC supplies and the front-to-back (AFO) and back-to-front
  (AFI) airflow: QFX5100-48S-AFO, -AFI, -DC-AFO, -DC-AFI, -3AFO and -3AFI, the
  Junos Fusion QFX5100-48SH-AFO and -AFI, and QFX5100-48T-AFO, -AFI, -DC-AFO,
  -DC-AFI and the QFX5100-48TH-AFO and -AFI. Each part number has a NetBox and
  a Nautobot device type.
- Juniper field-replaceable units for them: `juniper/qfx5100-fan-afo@1` and
  `juniper/qfx5100-fan-afi@1` (QFX5100-FAN-AFO and -AFI), and the 650 W
  supplies `juniper/qfx5100-jpsu-650w-ac-afo@1`, `-ac-afi@1`, `-dc-afo@1` and
  `-dc-afi@1` (JPSU-650W-AC-AFO, -AC-AFI, -DC-AFO and -DC-AFI).
