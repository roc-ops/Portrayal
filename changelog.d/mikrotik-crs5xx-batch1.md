### Added
- MikroTik's Cloud Router Switch CRS5xx, batch 1: `mikrotik/crs518-16xs-2xq-rm` (sixteen 25G SFP28 and two 100G QSFP28
  cages, a 100 Megabit management port, an RJ45 console and a USB type A port, with two AC supplies and four fans that the
  brochure calls hot-swap but gives no part number) and `mikrotik/crs510-8xs-2xq-in` (eight SFP28 and two QSFP28 cages,
  a management port with PoE in and an RJ45 console, with two hot-swap AC supplies on the front and two fans, 320 mm wide
  with rack ears in the box), both from MikroTik's dimensions drawings. The supplies and fans are part of the chassis, not
  bays, because no held document prints a part number for them. The CRS504-4XQ-IN and the CRS520-4XS-16XQ-RM are not in
  this batch.
- The CRS518's top-edge vent is drawn as separate V and lambda openings with the `motif` pattern. The CRS510 places its
  DC jack (`common/dc-barrel@1`, carrying the voltage and no current) and its two-pin DC terminal
  (`common/terminal-header-508-2@3`) as parts, with the bracket, voltage legend and polarity marks printed, draws its
  supply vents as slots, and both devices' fans show a plain hub.
- Both devices are drawn again from MikroTik's photographs: grey jack shells, the USB port's silver shell, the grey divider
  in each SFP28 stack, the printed brackets and a larger model name, steel handles, latches and screws on the supplies and
  fans, and the CRS510's lettering at its printed size. The CRS510's earth stud is a placed part and its face colour
  follows the front photograph. The cage frames, the inlet pins' colour and the earth symbol's colour are declared as gaps.
