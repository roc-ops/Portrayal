### Added
- `generic/dvi-d-plug@1`, the cable end of a DVI digital jack: a male plug that mates the `dvi-d` key, drawn from the Digital Display
  Working Group's Digital Visual Interface specification, Revision 1.0 (Figure 5-5): the 39.50 by 15.13 outline, the D-shaped shell
  24.16 by 7.90, the two jackscrews 32.51 apart and the 24 contact positions of three rows of eight at a 0.075 inch pitch. The hood
  has no form beyond its outline, the shell's depth, the cable's diameter and the colours are estimates, and each says so in the
  part. Which of the 24 contacts a cable populates is not stated by the specification and is not claimed. The contacts are not
  drawn from the cable end. They and the flat blade slot are drawn on the rear face, `generic/dvi-d-plug-mating@1`, at the figure's
  printed grid, with the figure's numerals, its second outline line, the shell's lip line and the jackscrews' crosshairs; the contact
  size, the slot and those extra lines are estimated from the figure and say so in the part.

### Changed
- The `dvi-d` registry entry and the `dvi-d` standards entry no longer say that no plug part is in the library or that the
  specification is not held; the standards entry gains `dvi-d-plug` and records what the specification prints for the receptacle's
  shell, its panel cutout and its contact pitch beside the jack's own estimated figures. The jack itself is unchanged. One row of
  the port records is closed: the DVI-D jack's slot has a plug to mate it.
