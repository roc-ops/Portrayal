### Changed
- `cable-types.json` (table version 1.4.0) states a jacket colour for two
  more named types, the Cisco StackPower cables `cab-spwr-30cm` and
  `cab-spwr-150cm` (`#304548`, `basis: photo`, counted on the product image
  in the Catalyst 9300 Series Switches Hardware Installation Guide). A rack
  cable that names either type is drawn in that colour where it was drawn in
  the `power` media's black, in the rack views, the sheets and the draw.io
  export. A cable with a colour of its own, or whose two seated ends agree on a
  jacket colour, is drawn as before. A bare media type still states none. A
  reader that does not know the key ignores it.
