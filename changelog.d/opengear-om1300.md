### Added
- Opengear OM1300 operations managers: the OM1304-4E, OM1304-4E-C, OM1308-8E
  and OM1308-8E-C as four devices with a front and a rear drawn from the
  maker's photographs and the data sheet envelope. Each has four or eight RJ45
  serial ports stated as console server ports, a block of as many managed
  switch jacks, two combo network ports drawn as an SFP cage and an RJ45 jack
  under one number, two USB-A ports, a USB-C console port, an erase pinhole,
  six lamps and two 12 VDC power jacks; the -C models add four SMA antenna jacks
  and two SIM slots. Every position read off a photograph is declared an
  estimate with its method, and no rack ear or kit is drawn because no document
  dimensions one. The devices reuse existing parts. This closes no rows of the
  port records, and no existing device, part or port changes.
