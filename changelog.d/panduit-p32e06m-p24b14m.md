### Added
- Two Panduit rack PDUs: the P32E06M G5 intelligent PDU (monitored and switched, single phase, 24 IEC C13 and 8 IEC C19 in four blocks
  of eight, two single-pole breakers and a controller between the halves) and the P24B14M basic PDU (three-phase delta, 12 IEC C13 and
  12 IEC C19 in six blocks of four, each block on its own double-pole breaker). Every face the maker's drawings show is drawn: the
  outlet face, the rear with its two mounting buttons 1555 mm apart, the cord end with the fixed IEC 60309 cord and the far end. The long
  sides are left empty, with the reason stated, and no photograph of either unit is held, so no colour is checked against one. New
  parts: `panduit/g5-breaker-20a`, `panduit/g5-breaker-pair-20a`, `panduit/g5-controller`, `panduit/g5-cord-60309-2pe-6h`,
  `panduit/g5-cord-60309-3pe-9h` and `panduit/g5-mounting-button`. The two cords export as an `other` power port labelled with the plug.
  The outlets are the library's C13 and C19 wells, and the controller's five RJ45-style jacks state no function because no document
  held labels them. The controller states no power draw, which lint reports.
