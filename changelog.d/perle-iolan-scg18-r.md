### Added
- The Perle console server `perle/iolan-scg18-r`, the third R model of the IOLAN SCG line beside the SCG50 R and
  SCG34 R: the dual-AC chassis with 16 RJ45 RS-232 serial ports in its first G16 module bay, six faces drawn from the
  maker's installation guide (the mechanical drawing for the outline and the front, the labelled photographs for the
  rear), and its two empty module bays declared. It uses the parts the other two already use and adds no part and no
  registry key. The positions on the rear are read off a photograph and are estimates, and the second bay's place is
  an estimate from the module pitch. It closes no records row.
