### Added
- `juniper/mpc1-3d-q@1` (MX-MPC1-3D-Q, 175 W), `juniper/mpc2e-3d-q@1` (MX-MPC2E-3D-Q, 294 W), `juniper/mpc2e-3d-p@1`
  (MX-MPC2E-3D-P, 294 W), `juniper/mpc2e-3d-ng-q@1` (MX-MPC2E-3D-NG-Q, 529 W) and `juniper/mpc3e-3d-ng-q@1`
  (MX-MPC3E-3D-NG-Q, 583 W): the queuing, P and NG-queuing MPC carriers, each with two MIC bays taking the MICs of
  its own column in the module reference's MIC/MPC compatibility tables. The MPC1 Q card also stands for MX-MPC1E-3D-Q,
  and the MPC2E Q card for MX-MPC2-3D-Q, MX-MPC2-3D-EQ and MX-MPC2E-3D-EQ, which print the same watts and the same
  MIC column. Every slot of the MX240, MX480 and MX960 that takes the MPC1E, MPC2E, MPC2E NG or MPC3E NG now takes
  the matching card too, a minor on each chassis (now 6.6.0, 7.5.0 and 6.2.0); nothing placed moves. The cards are
  drawn from the reference's figures, not photographed, and the three chassis list their faceplates as an open gap.
