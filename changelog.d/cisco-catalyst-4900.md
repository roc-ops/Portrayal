### Added
- Cisco's Catalyst 4900 family, starting with the Catalyst 4948E-F (`cisco/ws-c4948e-f`): the back-to-front
  build of the 4948E, a 1RU switch with 48 10/100/1000 RJ-45 ports and four 1/10G SFP+ ports. Its front is
  the 4948E's (estimated, as the guide shows the two alike) and its rear is its own, with two blue
  PWR-C49E-300AC-F supplies and the WS-X4993-F fan tray. WS-C4948E-F-S and WS-C4948E-F-E are one orderable
  configuration.
- The 4948E-F's field-replaceable units, family-prefixed by Cisco part number: `cisco/pwr-c49e-300ac-f`,
  `cisco/ws-x4993-f` and the blank power supply cover `cisco/ws-x4994-f`.
- The 4948E-F's two rack-bracket positions, on the front sides and on the rear sides of the chassis.
- The DCIM device types for WS-C4948E-F-S and WS-C4948E-F-E, in the NetBox and Nautobot exports, and the
  module types for the three new parts.
