### Added
- Cisco Nexus 9000 fixed switches, batch 3: `cisco/n9k-c9336c-fx2-e` (Nexus 9336C-FX2-E, 36 x
  40/100G QSFP28, 1 RU, six fan modules), a device of its own and not a configuration of the
  9336C-FX2. It has port-side intake and port-side exhaust builds with 750 W AC or 1100 W DC
  supplies. One DCIM device type is exported: `N9K-C9336C-FX2-E`. New components: the supplies
  `cisco/nxa-pac-750w` and `cisco/nxa-pdc-1100w`. The Nexus 9336C-FX2, 93240YC-FX2 and
  93216TC-FX2 are not yet modelled.
