### Added
- The maker Perle, and two console servers of its IOLAN SCG line: `perle/iolan-scg50-r` and `perle/iolan-scg34-r`, the
  dual-AC R models with 48 or 32 RJ45 RS-232 serial ports, each with six faces drawn from the maker's installation guide
  (the mechanical drawing for the outline and the front, the labelled photographs for the rear). Five parts of the
  maker's own: the display, the keypad, the power button, the Micro SD slot and the rocker switch. A new connector
  registry key, `scg-g16-module`, for the G16 module bay, stated by the one empty bay of the SCG34 R. The modules are
  not modelled; the positions on the rear are read off a photograph and are estimates. It closes no records row.
