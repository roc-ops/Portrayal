### Added
- The Juniper QFX10002-60C (`juniper/qfx10002-60c`, #39): a fixed 2RU switch with 60 QSFP28 ports in two bands of
  30, its port lamps, its management panel (USB, status lamps, RJ-45 management and console, the MGMT and PTP SFP
  ports, 1PPS and 10 MHz), and its rear with four power supply bays numbered down the columns, three fan bays and the
  ESD point. The AC build (QFX10002-60C), the DC build (QFX10002-60C-DC) and the spare chassis (JNP10002-60C) are
  orderable configurations. It seats the QFX10002 family's 1600 W supplies and supply cover as they are.
- The QFX10002-60C's fan module, JNP10002-FAN1 (`juniper/qfx10002-jnp10002-fan1`), its own part beside the 72Q's.
- The DCIM device types for the three orderable builds, in the NetBox and Nautobot exports, and the module type for
  JNP10002-FAN1.
