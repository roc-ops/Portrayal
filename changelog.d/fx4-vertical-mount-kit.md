### Added
- **`nokia/fx4-vertical-air-intake-tray` and `nokia/fx4-vertical-top-baffle`**: the two
  parts of the 7360 ISAM FX-4 vertical shelf mount kit that take rack units, each placed
  as a device of two units under and over a shelf rolled 90 (`nokia/nfxs-f-bb`, which stands
  11 units tall rolled). Sizes are from the kit's unit data sheet; the units are read from the
  installation manual's Figure 144. The kit's part numbers are not the FX-8 kit's. The
  baffle's ring-guides are not drawn. No record of the conformance list is closed.
