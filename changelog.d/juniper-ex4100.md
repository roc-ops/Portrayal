### Added
- Juniper EX4100-24T, EX4100-24P, EX4100-48T and EX4100-48P (#39): fixed 1RU Gigabit access switches with four SFP28
  Virtual Chassis ports and four SFP+ uplinks, from the EX4100 Switch Hardware Guide (2026-10-01). Each model is one
  device. The EX4100-24T-DC, EX4100-48T-DC and EX4100-48T-AFI are configurations of it, and each exports as a DCIM device
  type. The fan modules (EX4100-FAN-AFO and -AFI), the 150 W AC (AFO and AFI), 150 W DC and 920 W AC power supplies and
  the cover panel for an empty supply slot are bays, with new `juniper/ex4100-*` components.
