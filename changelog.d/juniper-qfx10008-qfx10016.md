### Added
- Juniper QFX10008 (13RU) and QFX10016 (21RU) (#39): the modular chassis of the QFX10000 family, from the QFX10008 and
  QFX10016 Switch Hardware Guides (2025-12-11). Each model is one device. Its base, redundant, high-voltage AC and DC
  builds are configurations (QFX10008-BASE, -BASE-H, -REDUND, -REDUND-H and -REDUND-DC; QFX10016-BASE, -REDUND and
  -REDUND-DC), and each exports as a DCIM device type. The two Routing and Control Boards, the power supplies, the fan
  trays and the covers are components in bays: the QFX10000-RE, QFX10000-PWR-AC, QFX10000-PWR-DC, JNP10K-PWR-DC2,
  QFX10008-FAN and QFX10016-FAN are new, and so is `juniper/jnp10k-pwr-ac2@2`, the JNP10K-PWR-AC2 drawn to the guides'
  3.6 x 3.5 in. The QFX10008 seats version 2. The QFX10016 seats version 1, the MX10008's, and the QFX10008 its
  JNP10008-FAN and -FAN2, as they are. The QFX10000 line cards are left out; the line-card bays seat the cover.
