### Added
- `nvidia/a100-pcie-80gb@1`, the NVIDIA A100 80GB PCIe card: a passive
  dual-slot full-height full-length GPU. Its face is the two-slot bracket with
  one vent window and no ports, drawn from a straight-on photograph of the
  card. Its 3D body carries the board, the edge connector, the backplate, the
  heat-sink shroud, three NVLink caps on the top edge and the CPU 8-pin power
  connector, at the lengths of NVIDIA's product brief PB-10577. It exports a
  NetBox and a Nautobot module type. Nothing seats it yet, because no bay in
  the library spans two slots.
