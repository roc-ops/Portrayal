### Added
- The Eaton Tripp Lite series B097-048, a 48-port serial console server for a desktop or a 19 inch
  rack (`eaton/b097-048` 0.1.0). The Rear carries the 48 RJ45 serial ports in two rows of 24 and
  three groups of eight columns, numbered as the panel prints them, with the Cisco straight pin-out,
  the two RJ45 Ethernet ports NET1 and NET2 and the two IEC C14 inlets of its two built-in supplies,
  each with its rocker switch; the Front carries the RJ45 console port, two USB-A ports, the erase
  pinhole and four labelled status lamps. It is drawn from existing parts and one new one, the small
  rocker switch `eaton/b097-power-rocker` 1.0.0, and every position is estimated because the maker
  publishes only two photographs of the faces. Where the maker's sources disagree (the width and depth
  of the body, the weight, the storage temperature, the number of USB ports, the patch cables) the
  device says so in its gaps and states no weight. One device adds one version record and two DCIM
  device-type files; no existing device or part changes and no record of the conformance list closes.
