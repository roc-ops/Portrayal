### Changed
- The alarm relay headers and nrgNET connectors on the rear centre blocks of the Amphenol Network Solutions
  300CB08, nrgSMART and nrgILS power panels, and the four RJ45 ports of the Dell 14G network daughter card,
  now join a port group, so a port on a block or card placed in a device says what it is for: the alarm
  headers and the nrgNET connectors are `management`, the network ports are `traffic`. No group states a
  speed: the panel documents print no rate for a relay contact and tie none to an nrgNET jack, and the
  daughter card's two 10 Gb and two 1 Gb ports keep the speed each already states. Part ids, positions and drawn geometry are
  unchanged; each of the four contracts takes a minor, and the twelve devices that place one take a patch.
  The change closes no conformance record.
