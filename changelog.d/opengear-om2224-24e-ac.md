### Added
- Opengear OM2224-24E, OM2224-24E-L, OM2224-24E-10G and OM2224-24E-10G-L operations managers on dual AC power. Each is a new 1RU device.
  The front has 24 RJ45 serial ports, a 24-port managed switch, an RJ45 console, a micro-USB console and two USB 3.0 ports.
  The gigabit models have two combination SFP and RJ45 network ports. The 10G models have two SFP+ cages and one RJ45 port.
  The rear has eight USB 2.0 ports and two IEC C14 inlets. The cellular models add two SIM slots and three antenna jacks.
  Size is the data sheet's. Positions are read off the maker's pictures, so they are estimates with their method written in each device.
  The serial lines are console server ports, so the NetBox and Nautobot device types list them under `console-server-ports`.
  No existing device, part, port or export changes. This closes no row of the conformance records.
