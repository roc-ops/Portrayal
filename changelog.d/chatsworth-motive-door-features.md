### Changed
- The door of each of the 10 Chatsworth Motive single-sided vertical cable managers (32610 to 32614, each in black and in
  Glacier White) now draws what the maker's pictures show of it: two vertical seams that divide the front into three panels
  and a plate down its upper part, centred (silver on the white parts, a lighter grey on the black ones), with the place
  of the maker's mark reserved at the plate's top and left unpainted. The figures are estimates read from the data
  sheet's page 1 photograph, which shows one 10 in Glacier White part; they are taken as fractions of the door's width
  on every width and in both colours.
- The back of each door is drawn on the part's rear, left and right faces: the two blue latches the installation
  instructions describe, a housing and a round handle at each side edge, about half way down. Their sizes are stand-ins.
  The tip of each handle shows at the door's edge on the front.
- Each door component takes a minor version and each device a patch version. The `door-features` gap of each device is
  replaced by a `door-hook` gap, because no held picture shows the hook on the inside of the front door.
- This closes no row of the cable-manager list.

### Added
- A test that the door skin draws the seams and the plate, that the mark's place is reserved without paint, and that the
  device draws the latches and keeps only the hook as a gap.
