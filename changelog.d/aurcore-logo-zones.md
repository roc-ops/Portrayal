### Changed
- AurCore: the ten industrial switches (AIS2001, AIS2001P, AIS2002, AIS2002P, AIS2003, AIS2003P, AIS2004,
  AIS2004P, AIS4001, AIS4001P) reserve the place of the printed logotype on their fronts as a `logo-zone`
  region, measured on square-on product photographs. No drawing moves and the plain text AURCORE stays.
  Each takes a patch version (2.1.1), so the exports' `Drawing version` line moves with it. The
  AIS2001P's right side also prints the logotype, but it is shown only in a three-quarter photograph with
  no scale, so no place is reserved there.
