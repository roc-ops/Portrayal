### Added
- Juniper EX6210 (#39): the end-of-life 14 U modular campus switch, from the hardware guide (2020-12-15), as one device
  with ten line card and SRE slots over four power supply slots and a rear fan tray. The four shipping orders
  (EX6210-S64-96T-A25, EX6210-S64-96P-A25, EX6210-S64-96P-A50 and EX6210-S64-96T-D21) and the spare chassis
  (EX6210-CHAS) are configurations, each exporting as a DCIM device type; a base build and a redundant example with
  two SRE modules are also drawn. The SRE module (EX6200-SRE64-4XS), the 2500 W AC, 5000 W AC and 2100 W DC power
  supplies, the fan tray (EX6210-FAN), the cover panels and the power supply shelf are components in bays. The three
  EX6200 line cards (EX6200-48T, EX6200-48P and EX6200-48F) are not modelled: their slots show cover panels.
