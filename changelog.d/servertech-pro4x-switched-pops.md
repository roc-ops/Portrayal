### Added
- The Server Technology PRO4X Switched POPS 4G16A, a 0U rack PDU with 36 outlets (12 HDOT C13 and 24 HDOT Cx)
  in two banks either side of a controller panel, six branch breakers and the back's mounting positions, drawn
  from the maker's outline drawing and wiring diagram. The HDOT Cx outlet is a new part with its own connector
