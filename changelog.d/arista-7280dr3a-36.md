  DCS-7280DR3AK-36S-F), and a DC build is shown as a worked example.

  supplies PWR-2411-AC-RED and PWR-2411-DC-RED (`arista/7280dr3a-pwr-2400-ac`, `arista/7280dr3a-pwr-2400-dc`).
- The DCIM device types for the four 7280DR3A-36 builds and the module types for the fan and the two supplies, in the NetBox and
  Nautobot exports.
