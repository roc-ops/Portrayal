### Added
- The Eaton ePDU G3 basic EPBZ77, a 0U rack PDU with 24 NEMA 5-20R outlets in one row on a fixed 15 ft L5-20P
  cord, and the Eaton Tripp Lite series PDUMV30, a 0U metered rack PDU with 24 NEMA 5-15/20R outlets in two
  breakered banks, two bank meters and a fixed 10 ft L5-30P cord. Each is drawn from the maker's drawing, every
  face. Three new parts: the EPBZ77's fixed cord, the PDUMV30's fixed cord and its two-digit bank meter. The
  L5-30P cord exports as an `other` power port labelled NEMA L5-30P. The change closes no rows of the
  port-usable records.
