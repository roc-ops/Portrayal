### Changed
- The APC by Schneider Electric NetShelter AP7821B and AP7921B 1U rack PDUs now draw the small round marks their data
  sheet photographs show at the ends of the outlet face (two at each end) and on the display end (six in two rows of three).
  Every position is an estimate read off a 369 px photograph, and the devices say so. The AP7821B's display end borrows
  the AP7921B's pattern. Each device takes a patch version, which moves the version line of its DCIM device types. No
  record row is closed.
