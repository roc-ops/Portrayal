### Added
- Juniper EX2300-24T, EX2300-24P, EX2300-48T and EX2300-48P (#39): fixed 1RU Gigabit access switches with four
  SFP+/SFP uplinks, from the EX2300 Switch Hardware Guide (2025-08-12). Each model is one device. Its -TAA and -VC
  part numbers, and the EX2300-24T-DC, are configurations of it, and each exports as a DCIM device type. The power
  supply and fans are built into the chassis, so no bay is modelled.
