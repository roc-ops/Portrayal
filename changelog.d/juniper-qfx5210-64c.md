### Added
- Juniper QFX5210-64C (#39): a 2RU spine switch with 64 QSFP28 ports in sixteen columns of four and two SFP+ ports,
  from the QFX5210 Switch Hardware Guide (2026-03-09). It is one device. The AC and DC, front-to-back (AIR OUT) and
  back-to-front (AIR IN) builds are configurations of it, and the AIR OUT, AIR IN and chassis-spare part numbers
  (QFX5210-64C-AFO, -AFI and -CHAS) each export as a DCIM device type. The four fan modules and the two 1100 W AC or DC
  power supplies are components in bays, so the explorer and a rack can show them seated, swapped or absent.
