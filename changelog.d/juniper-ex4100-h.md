### Added
- Juniper EX4100-H-12MP, EX4100-H-24MP and EX4100-H-24F (#39): the hardened (industrial), fanless EX4100-H access
  switches, from the EX4100-H Switch Hardware Guide (2026-10-01). Each model is one device. The 12MP is an upright
  desktop box with its two supplies outside the chassis; the 24MP and 24F are 1RU boxes with two field-replaceable
  supply slots, a 340 W or 90 W AC or DC supply in the first and a blank over the second. Each exports as a DCIM
  device type. New components: the 340 W and 90 W AC supplies, the 24MP and 24F DC supplies, the supply blank, the
  12MP's PSU connector, the dry contact alarm connector, the power terminal pad and the Mode button.
