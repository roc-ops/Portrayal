### Added
- StarTech.com 1U horizontal rack PDUs, each with six views: the 8NS8-RACK-MOUNT-PDU, the PDU08C13H and the RKPW081915. The 8NS8-RACK-MOUNT-PDU is a
  switched strip, 440 x 44 x 95.5 mm between its brackets: a 15 A push-to-reset breaker and eight numbered rocker switches under covers on the front,
  eight NEMA 5-15R outlets and a fixed 8 ft cord with a NEMA 5-15P plug on the rear. The PDU08C13H is a basic strip, 440 x 44 x 98.8 mm: eight IEC C13 outlets,
  two round push buttons and a C20 inlet on the front; no document shows its rear. The RKPW081915 is a basic strip, 440 x 42.7 x 95.5 mm: a SURGE lamp, a GROUND
  lamp and a red rocker on the front; its rear is not shown, and the eight outlets and the 6 ft cord are placed there from the data sheet's counts, at estimated
  positions. The body width between the brackets, every position on the PDU08C13H and RKPW081915 fronts, and the outlet pitch are estimates, declared in each device. This closes no row of the port records.
- Parts for them: `startech/outlet-switch-cover@1` (a lit rocker under its hinged cover) and `startech/pdu-cord-5-15p@1` (the strain relief of a fixed cord with a
  NEMA 5-15P plug, exported as a `nema-5-15p` power port). Their sizes are declared estimates in each contract.
