### Added
- Opengear OM2224-24E-DDC and OM2224-24E-10G-DDC operations managers on dual DC power. Each is a new 1RU device.
  The front has 24 RJ45 serial ports, a 24-port managed switch, an RJ45 console, a micro-USB console and two USB 3.0
  ports. The 1G model has two combination SFP and RJ45 network ports. The 10G model has two SFP+ cages and one RJ45
  port. The rear has eight USB 2.0 ports and two DC input connectors. Size is the data sheet's.
  Positions are read off the maker's photographs, so they are estimates with their method written in each device.
  The serial lines are console server ports, so the NetBox and Nautobot device types list them under
  `console-server-ports`. The DC connectors are drawn as estimated art and export no power port, because no registered
  connector fits them. The AC and cellular models and the 16, 32 and 48 port models are not included. No existing
  device, part, port or export changes. This closes no row of the conformance records.
