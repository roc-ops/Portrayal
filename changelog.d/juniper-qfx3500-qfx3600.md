### Added
- Juniper (`juniper/qfx3500-48s4q`): the QFX3500-48S4Q, a 1RU data-centre switch with 48 SFP+ access
  ports and 4 QSFP+ uplink ports. Three orderable configurations: QFX3500-48S4Q-AFO (the default),
  -AFI and -ACR. Two example configurations seat DC supplies.
- Juniper (`juniper/qfx3600-16q`): the QFX3600-16Q, a 1RU data-centre switch with 16 QSFP+ ports and
  four management ports. Two orderable configurations: QFX3600-16Q-AFO (the default) and -AFI. Two
  example configurations seat DC supplies.
- The QFX3500 and QFX3600 field-replaceable units, each its own component: the fan trays
  `juniper/qfx3500-fan-afo@1` and `juniper/qfx3500-fanai@1`, the management boards
  `juniper/qfx3500-mb@1`, `juniper/qfx3500-mb-rj45-afo@1`, `juniper/qfx3500-mb-sfp-afi@1` and
  `juniper/qfx3500-mb-sfp-afo@1`, and the fan trays `juniper/qfx3600-qfxc01-fanbo@1` and
  `juniper/qfx3600-qfxc01-fanbi@1`. Both chassis seat the 650 W supplies of the QFX5100
  (`juniper/qfx5100-jpsu-650w-*@1`), which carry the same Juniper part numbers.
