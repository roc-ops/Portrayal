### Added
- StarTech.com rack cable managers (first slice of #1098, part of #1091), modelled from the
  maker's own dimensioned drawings and data sheets, with `startech` added to the vendor
  registry (listed after `celestica`). Eight parts: the finger ducts `startech/cmduct1ux`
  (1U) and `cmduct2u` (2U), each with a `duct` guide at the drawn 11 mm slot, fifteen slots in
  each of its two walls, three pass-throughs in its plate and a cover that ships fitted and
  comes off in a `cover-off` configuration; the D-ring panels `cablmanagerh` (five rings and
  four 29 mm holes) and `cablmanager2` (five removable rings, shown off in `rings-off`); the
  brush panel `cmbrush1u` (a `brush` pass across its window, a standalone brush panel);
  and the D-ring hooks `cmhook1u`, `cmhook1un` and `cmhookmw` (D-ring hook singles, each a
  rack-face part placed on one rail with `side`, `cmhookmw` at 1.5 U). Hook-and-loop strips
  are for a later slice. No cable capacity is stated: no source gives one.
