### Added
- Opengear CM8100 console managers on AC power: CM8116, CM8132 and CM8148 with a gigabit uplink, and CM8116-10G,
  CM8132-10G and CM8148-10G with two 10G SFP+ cages and two 1G RJ45 ports. Each is a new 1RU device with 16, 32 or 48
  RJ45 serial ports, a front RJ45 console and two USB 3.0 ports, and two IEC C14 inlets on the rear. Size and weight
  are the data sheets'; positions are read off the maker's photographs and quick start guide, so they are estimates
  with their method written in each device. The serial lines are console server ports, so the NetBox and Nautobot
  device types list them under `console-server-ports`. The DC versions, the cellular versions and the 96-port
  CM8196-10G are not included. No existing device, part, port or export changes. This closes no row of the
  conformance records.
