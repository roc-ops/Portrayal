### Changed
- The Eaton Tripp Lite series B097-016 is redrawn from the maker's two 1000 pixel photographs
  (`eaton/b097-016` 0.2.0, a minor: every existing id and port stays, five ids are added). The rear
  gains the rocker switch above each inlet (the existing `eaton/b097-power-rocker`) and the number
  printed over and under each serial jack, odd on top and even below. The front gains the PWR, H/B,
  SER and NET legends, the erase pinhole with its label, and the maker's mark and model line. No held
  document for this model names the inlet type, so the C14 inlet is now declared from the rear
  photograph and said so in a gap, instead of citing the owner's manual, which says only "IEC". Every
  position is still estimated. The change adds a new test and closes no record of the conformance list.
