### Added
- Registry keys `st` and `terminal-250-3`, and four parts that present or mate them: `common/st-receptacle@1` and `generic/st-plug@1` (the ST bayonet fibre receptacle and its plug), `common/terminal-header-250-3@1` and `generic/terminal-250-3-plug@1` (a three-position pluggable terminal block on a 2.50 mm pitch and a stand-in for its cable plug). The Adtran OSA 5422 data sheet (IRIG/TIMECODE line card) names both connectors for the Oscilloquartz IRIG-B card: "ST connector - fiber 62/125um" for the optical output and "WR-TBL 3-pin Serie 382 - 2.50 mm" for the alarm/pulse relay. No dimension is held for either, so the four parts are drawn at the sizes the card's own art measured and say so.

### Changed
- `oscilloquartz/irig-b@1` 1.0.0 to 1.1.0: the optical output and the alarm/pulse relay are placed parts under the ids the elements had (`optical-out`, `alarm-pulse`), so they present a registered interface. Each joins a group of its own. The relay's contacts are drawn on the data sheet's 2.50 mm pitch where the card drew them about 3.4 apart. The isolated IRIG input and the ten IRIG outputs stay elements: no page held names their connectors.
- The four Oscilloquartz devices that seat the card take a patch (0.1.4 to 0.1.5) with their locks and the drawing version line of their exported device types, and the IRIG-B module types carry the new contract version. Their gap `optical-irig-connector` is replaced by `irig-card-connectors`, which names the two connectors still unnamed.
- The sentence of the port-usable records row for `osa-5420-quartz` names the two ports that remain. No row is closed and `cap:` does not move.
