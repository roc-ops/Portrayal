### Added
- The Moxa NPort 5650-16 and NPort 5650-16-T rackmount serial device servers as two devices (`moxa/nport-5650-16`,
  `moxa/nport-5650-16-t`). Each draws the front, the rear and the top. The front has the two rows of 16 Tx and Rx lamps,
  the Ready, LAN and PWR lamps and the Reset button; the standard-temperature unit adds the LCD panel and four push
  buttons, which the wide-temperature unit does not have. The rear has the ground screw, the AC inlet and its rocker
  switch, the LAN jack and the 16 RJ45 serial ports in two groups of eight. Every position is read off the maker's
  dimensioned data sheet drawing, to the 440 mm body, and no photograph is held, so no colour is compared with one. No
  rack ear or kit is drawn because no held document positions one. The sides and the underside have no drawing of a
  16-port unit and are declared empty.
- New parts `moxa/nport-5600-lcm@1`, `moxa/nport-5600-keys@1` and `moxa/nport-5600-power-rocker@1`, and the maker `moxa`
  in the vendor registry. The LCD panel states no power figure because no held document gives one. This closes no rows
  of the port records. No existing device, part or port changes.
