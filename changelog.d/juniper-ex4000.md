### Added
- Juniper EX4000-48P, EX4000-48T, EX4000-48MP, EX4000-24P, EX4000-24T, EX4000-24MP, EX4000-12P, EX4000-12T, EX4000-12MP
  and EX4000-8P (#39): the ten fixed access switches of the EX4000 line, from the EX4000 Switch Hardware Guide (2026-01-20)
  and its datasheet. Each model is one device with one configuration carrying its part number, and each exports as a DCIM
  device type. The power supply and the fans are built into every chassis, so no bay is modelled; the 8- and 12-port models
  are fanless desk units 264 mm wide.
