### Added
- The APC by Schneider Electric NetShelter AP9571A basic 1U rack PDU. It has ten IEC C13 outlets in two banks of five and a rocker
  circuit breaker before each bank. A fixed input cord ends in a NEMA L6-30P plug. The outlet face and the plain face are drawn from the
  maker's data sheet and product photographs. The two ends carry four faint marks each. The top and the bottom say what
  was searched. The photographs are small, so every position on the outlet face is an
  estimate and the device says so. Two new parts of the maker: `apc/pdu-cord-l6-30p@1`, the cord's boot, and
  `apc/pdu-breaker-rocker@1`, the rocker breaker. The DCIM export gains a power-port type for the cord, `nema-l6-30p`, as the other
  fixed cords carry. The device and the parts are new, so nothing that exists changes; two DCIM device types are new, one each for
  NetBox and Nautobot. No record row is closed.
