### Added
- Juniper QFX5250-64OE-L (#39): the liquid-cooled 2OU 1.6T data-centre switch for an Open Rack v3 rack, from the
  QFX5250 Switch Hardware Guide (2026-06-17). It is the one QFX5250 model the guide describes. The front has 64
  OSFP ports and two SFP28 ports, each with its printed number and lamp, the management, console, USB, timing,
  ON/OFF and reset parts and the LEAKAGE, ID, SYS and ALM lamps; the rear has the two coolant plugs and the
  busbar connector. The switch has no power supply and no fan module, so no bay is modelled. It exports as one
  DCIM device type.
