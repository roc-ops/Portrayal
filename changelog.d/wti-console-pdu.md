### Added
- WTI CPM-800-1, CPM-1600-1 and DSM-8 console servers, each with front and rear drawn and the four other views stated as not drawn. The CPM
  models are console server and PDU hybrids: eight or sixteen RJ45 serial ports, two Ethernet jacks, a phone jack and a USB set-up port on the
  front, and on the rear two or four IEC C20 inlets feeding eight or sixteen NEMA 5-15R outlets in banks of four. The DSM-8 carries two C14
  inlets, eight RJ45 serial ports and two Ethernet jacks on the back. Positions are read from the maker's dimensional drawings, which give no
  connector dimension, and every colour is read from a data sheet render and is not checked against a photograph. This closes no row of the
  port records. The maker is registered as `wti`.
