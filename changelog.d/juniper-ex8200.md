### Added
- Juniper EX8208 and EX8216 (#39): the two end-of-life modular EX8200 chassis, 14 U and 21 U, from their hardware guides
  (both PDFs dated 2020-12-16) and the June 2015 datasheets. Each is one device with its slots drawn: eight and sixteen
  line card slots, the SRE0, SF and SRE1 slots of the EX8208 and the RE0 and RE1 slots and eight rear SF slots of the EX8216,
  six power supply slots and the fan tray (two on the EX8216), which carries the slot numbers. The shipping orders
  (EX8208-BASE-AC, -BASE-AC3, -REDUND-AC, -REDUND-DC, EX8216-BASE-AC, -REDUND-AC, -REDUND-AC2, -REDUND-DC) and the spare
  chassis (EX8208-CHAS-S, EX8216-CHAS-S) are configurations, each exporting as a DCIM device type. The SRE and RE modules,
  SF modules, fan trays, the 2000 W and 3000 W AC supplies and the 2000 W and 3000 W DC supplies are components in bays, and
  so are the line card, RE and power supply cover panels. The EX8200 line cards are not modelled: their slots show covers.
