### Added
- Two more Panduit rack PDUs. The P42G20G is a G6 intelligent PDU, three-phase delta, monitored and switched per outlet. It has 21 C13/C15 and
  21 C13/C15/C19 combination sockets and six double-pole breakers. The P24B32M is a basic half-height PDU with 18 IEC C13 and 6 IEC C19 outlets.
  Its six breakers and its earth screw are on the rear, the side opposite the outlets.
- Each face is drawn from what its maker drawing shows. Marks are read from the sheets and declared estimated. No photograph of either unit is held.
- New part: `panduit/g6-controller`, the G6 controller. Its buttons, lamps, USB port and five RJ45 jacks carry the labels the drawing prints.
  The controller states no power draw, which lint reports.
