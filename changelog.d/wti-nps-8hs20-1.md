### Added
- WTI NPS-8HS20-1 network power switch (a switched PDU), 1U, with front and rear drawn. The front carries the RJ45 set-up port, ON and RDY
  lamps, DEFAULT and RESET buttons, eight output status lamps and two 20 A circuit breakers. The rear carries one IEC C20 inlet feeding
  eight NEMA 5-15R outlets, A1 to A8, two alarm lamps and a 10/100/1000 Ethernet jack. The size is from the maker's specification table
  and the positions are read from the line figures of the NPS Series Hardware Guide, which give no connector dimension, so every
  position is declared an estimate. The colours are read from the specification sheet's photograph of the unit. The two sides and the
  underside are plain outlines at the stated size, because no document shows their details. This closes no row of the port records.
