### Added
- `std/dvi-d-jack@2`, the DVI-D receptacle drawn from the Digital Display Working Group's Digital Visual Interface specification,
  Revision 1.0 (Figure 5-1). It draws the shell 24.03 by 7.72 and 24 contacts in three rows of eight at a 1.905 pitch.
  It draws 13.335 between the first contact and the eighth, and the six numerals the figure prints.
  The corners, the inner outline line, the cell and blade sizes and the first column's offset are estimated from the figure.
  The part says so. The colours are measured on the Raritan data sheet photograph.
  The first version stays as it is, with the photograph's 26.8 by 9.8.

### Changed
- The Raritan DSX2-32 (0.2.0 to 1.0.0) seats the second version of the jack.
  Its two screws now stand 32.51 apart, the distance the specification prints, centred on the jack.
  The jack's slot has a new size and place, so the version lock asks a major. The exported ports and interfaces do not move.
