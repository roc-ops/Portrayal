### Added
- The fixed input cord of the Server Technology PRO4X Switched POPS 4G16A as a part, `servertech/pro4x-cord-60309@1`:
  the cord-entry frame at the cord end, drawn from the outline drawing's end view, for the IEC 60309 3P+N+G 6h plug the
  maker's SKU page prints for C4G36RA-GPBD2FE1. It exports as an `other` power port labelled with the plug, and each of
  the PDU's 36 outlets names it as its feed. The change closes no rows of the port-usable records.

### Changed
