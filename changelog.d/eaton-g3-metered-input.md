### Added
- The Eaton ePDU G3 metered input rack PDUs EMIT03-10 and EMIT04-10, both 1U horizontal with 12 outlets in two
  colour-coded sections of six, each section on its own 20 A breaker, a hot-swappable meter module (two
  daisy-chain RJ-45 ports, a network RJ-45, a USB-A port, a pixel display and four buttons) and a fixed 10 ft
  cord. The EMIT03-10 has 12 NEMA 5-20R outlets on a NEMA L5-30P cord (120 V); the EMIT04-10 has 8 IEC C13 and
  4 IEC C19 outlets on a NEMA L6-30P cord (200 to 240 V). Each is drawn from the maker's specification drawing,
  every face. Six new parts: the two fixed cords, the one-pole and the two-pole section breakers, the meter's pixel
  display and its navigation button (four skins). The L5-30P cord exports as an `other` power port labelled NEMA
  L5-30P, as the PDUMV30's does; the L6-30P cord exports as `nema-l6-30p`. The change closes no rows of the
  port-usable records.
