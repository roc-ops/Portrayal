### Changed
- The Cisco Catalyst 2960S-24TD-L and 2960S-48TD-L (`cisco/ws-c2960s-24td` and `cisco/ws-c2960s-48td`, Refs #1275) draw their right wall.
  It has 18 openings: 7 small round holes, 6 peanut slots in two groups of three, and 5 small rectangles.
  One rectangle holds a light metal tab, which is drawn too.
- The right wall is not a copy of the left wall. Its pattern differs, and it has no rear cluster of slots.
  It is estimated from photographs of the product, a 24-port and a 48-port model, both 299 mm deep.
- The scale along the wall comes from the model's length, 299 mm, in Table 1-3 of the Cisco Catalyst 2960-S Switch Hardware Installation Guide.
  The scale across the wall comes from the printed 44.45 mm height.
  Each opening is good to about 3 mm along the wall and 2 mm across.
- The 48-port photograph's rack bracket hides the front 97 mm, which the 24-port photograph shows.
  A label on the 24-port photograph hides the wall from 223 to 280 mm, which the 48-port photograph shows.
- The 48TD applies the 24TD's pattern, as the left wall does, because the two models have the same length.
- The openings stay drawn as marks in a stand-in dark, and the wall keeps the device's colour.
  The lit rims of the round holes and the waists of the slots are left to a later touch.
- Each device takes a patch bump (1.0.3 to 1.0.4). No port, lamp or group id moves.
