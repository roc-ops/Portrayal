### Added
- **`common/ground-stud-pair-5-8-10-32@1`**: two 10-32 pan-head screws on 5/8 in.
  centres, presenting `stud-pair-5-8`, the sibling of the M4, M6 and 1/4-20
  pairs. A device places it where its documents name two 10-32 studs that a
  two-hole lug lands across.

### Changed
- **The earth studs of seven devices are parts a ground lug lands on**, where
  they were decor: the Nokia 7750 SR-12, SR-1e, SR-2e and SR-3e and the MikroTik
  CRS309-1G-8S+IN, CRS326-24G-2S+RM and CSS326-24G-2S+RM. The SR-1e, SR-2e and
  SR-3e place the new pair, the SR-12 places `common/ground-stud-pair-5-8-1-4@1`,
  and the three MikroTik switches place `common/ground-stud@1`. Each takes a
  minor, its lock and the `Drawing version` line of its exported device types,
  and no exported port moves. Pan-head screws and a hex nut stand in for the
  chassis studs and the wing nut, and each device says so in a gap. Lint L197 no
  longer warns on the seven.
