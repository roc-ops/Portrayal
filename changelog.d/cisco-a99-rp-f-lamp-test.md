### Changed
- `cisco/a99-rp-f@1` (2.1.1 to 2.2.0), the ASR 9902 and ASR 9903 route processor, models the LAMP TEST push button Cisco's
  drawing of the card shows at the right of its face: a `lamp-test` element of class `button`, the bezel and plunger drawn as
  two circles, the legend LAMP over TEST under and to the left of it, and a pushbutton relief. Its place is estimated from the
  drawing through two fitted axes, because the drawing's art box is not the contract's shape box. The five small apertures in
  an L beside the button stay unmodelled: no Cisco document held names them, and the contract's provenance says what was
  searched and what would settle it.
- The ASR 9902 (2.0.3 to 2.0.4) and ASR 9903 (2.0.4 to 2.0.5) each take a patch, because each seats two of these cards. The
  drawing version line of their exported device types moves with it, and the card's module types carry contract
  version 2.2.0.
