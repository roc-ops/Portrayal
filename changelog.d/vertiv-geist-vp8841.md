### Added
- The Vertiv Geist VP8841, a unit-level monitored vertical rack PDU, a 1778 mm strip 51 mm wide and deep.
  It has 36 C13 and 6 C19 outlets on the front in two circuits, each behind a 20 A double-pole breaker.
  A monitoring module sits between the circuits: two Ethernet jacks, a sensor jack, a serial jack, a USB port, a four-digit display and three keys.
  Positions are read from the maker's data sheet strip at about 2 mm per pixel.
  The fixed 10 ft cord exports as a power port of type other, labelled NEMA L6-30P.
  Three new parts: the module, the breaker and the cord ring. The back, the ends and the sides stay named gaps, as no held image shows them.
  The change closes no rows of the port-usable records.
