### Added
- The APC AR7710 cable containment bracket for NetShelter SX, SV and VX racks (#1097, part of #1091). It is modelled from
  APC's own data sheet and installation sheet. `apc/ar7710` is one bracket, 80 x 112 x 45 mm, a black sheet-steel cup open at both ends.
  It is `chassis.mount: rack-side` and is drawn on all six sides. The long face shows two keyholes, the slot between its
  two pieces and the hook in that slot. The rear shows the plate with its two screws and small stud. Each end face shows
  one keyhole. One screw shank and one small pin stand from the rear in a well 15 mm deep, estimated from the data
  sheet photograph. The bracket adds two parts, `apc/nsx-vcm-ar7710-base` and `apc/nsx-vcm-ar7710-screws`.
  It has no connector, and APC states no cable capacity. It closes no row of the conformance records.
