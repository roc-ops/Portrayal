### Added

- Dell PowerEdge R960 (`dell/r960`): the 4U four-socket 16th-generation rack server, modelled as a draft
  from Dell's installation and service manual, technical guide and spec sheet. Six fronts, each its own
  view with both control panels in the ears, the Express Service Tag and the honeycomb security bezel
  fitted over the drives: 32, 24, 16 and 8 x 2.5 inch, 16 x EDSFF E3.S, and 8 x 2.5 inch with 16 x E3.S.
  Five riser configurations (0-1, 0-2, 1, 2 and 3) over the four 86 mm power supply bays, the BOSS-N1
  bay, LOM, OCP and the rear I/O board, thirty builds in all, with their DCIM device types. A top view
  with the system board and the processor expansion module, four heatsinks, 64 DIMM slots named as Dell
  prints them, six fans, the shrouds, the support bar and the covers. The top view's fans, fan cage,
  heatsinks, shrouds, support bar, system cover and latch, supplies and risers, the supplies' depth and the
  security bezel's honeycomb are measured from Dell's R960 service model; the boards and the memory sockets'
  x are still read off the manual's figures, and where the two disagree the device records both. Riser
  configuration 3's rear is an estimate, and the 1800 W and -48 V DC supplies, direct liquid cooling and
  the LCD bezel are not modelled; see the device's gaps.
- Thirty-seven components under `dell/r960-`: the 2400 W and 2800 W 86 mm supplies, the 26 mm supply
  adapter and a supply blank, the rear I/O board, ten risers (1A, 1B, 1C, 2, 3A, 3B in two wirings, 4A,
  4B, 4C) with the riser 3B cage, the full-height and double-width slot fillers, the riser 2, riser 3 and
  VGA bracket blanks, the security bezel, the system board and PEM board, heatsink, fan, fan cage, air
  shroud top and bottom covers, support bar, system cover and drive cage cover, and the plans of the
  four risers and the supplies seen from above.
