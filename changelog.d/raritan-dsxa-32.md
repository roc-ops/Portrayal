### Added
- Raritan Dominion SX DSXA-32, the first-generation 32-port serial console server with dual AC power, one Ethernet port and a built-in modem.
  It is one device, 440 x 290 x 44 mm. The rear carries two C14 inlets with their switches, the modem jack and the DB9 terminal port.
  It also carries the Ethernet jack with three lamps, the reset hole and 32 RJ45 serial ports in four groups of two rows.
  Positions are read off photographs and are estimates. The front is drawn from the maker's oblique photograph of a unit labelled SX16.
  The front bezel and the side walls are drawn as hole and slot patterns, with one blue front lamp.
  The other thirteen first-generation models are not added. No row of the port records is closed.
