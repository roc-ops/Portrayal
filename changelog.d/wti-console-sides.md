### Changed
- WTI CPM-800-1, CPM-800-2, CPM-1600-1, CPM-1600-2 and DSM-8: the top and one side are now drawn from the maker's dimensional drawings. The top
  carries its cover screws (four, four, five, five and four) and the side carries its row of vent slots (31, 31, 39, 39 and 22), the cover plate
  and the screws, each with its cross recess. The CPM-800s and the DSM-8 also draw the front flange or block of the side. The drawings do not name the side they show, so it is drawn as the left and recorded as an estimate;
  the right side and the underside are still not drawn because no held page shows them. Positions are read from the drawings at about one
  millimetre. The CPM-1600 drawings print a cover depth of 12.50 inches but draw about 10.4, so the rear and the front features are placed from
  their own end. This closes no row of the port records.
