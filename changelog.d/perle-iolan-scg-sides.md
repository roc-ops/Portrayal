### Changed
- The three Perle console servers `perle/iolan-scg18-r`, `perle/iolan-scg34-r` and `perle/iolan-scg50-r` now draw
  their sides, top, bottom and front ears from the maker's mechanical drawing. Before, each side, the top and the
  bottom were a plain box and the front had no ears.
- Each side shows two rows of 32 vent slots and the ear bracket, with six round holes and six slotted ovals. It
  also shows two more round holes and two ringed holes. The top and bottom show the seam behind the front panel
  and the bracket legs.
- The drawing does not say which side it shows. The right side is drawn as the drawing shows it and the left is its
  mirror. Every figure is read off the drawing and is an estimate.
- The front is now 480.2 wide: the 436 body between two ear flanges, each with two slotted holes. The flanges are a
  new part, `perle/scg-rack-ear`. The chassis width and the top and bottom stay 436.
- Each device takes a major version, because every front position moves 22.1 mm to the right.
- Not drawn yet: the front marks and gold stripes. Also not drawn are the flanges seen edge-on on the top and
  bottom, and the 2.7 mm the rear inlets stand past the rear wall.
