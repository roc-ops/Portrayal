### Added
- Raritan PX3-5493 rack PDU, 0U, 52 x 65 x 1539 mm, with six views. It has 20 IEC C13 and 4 C19 outlets in four groups of six.
  It has two circuit breakers and a controller with two Ethernet jacks, three USB jacks and four RJ45 jacks. A fixed cord ends in an
  IEC 60309 2P+E 6h 32 A plug.
- The outlet places, the breaker plates and the controller panel are read off the maker's mechanical drawing to about 0.5 mm.
  Each outlet has a lamp, drawn off, with the states the user guide lists for the series. The two mounting buttons are 1244.0 mm apart.
- The two long sides and the ends are plain outlines. Colours, marks and printed text are estimates or not drawn, and the gaps say which.
  The device reuses parts already in the library and adds none.
