### Added
- The Vertiv Avocent ACS8000 advanced console server as four more devices, the AT&T cellular dual-AC units with 8, 16,
  32 and 48 RJ45 serial ports (`vertiv/acs8008-ln-dac-400`, `vertiv/acs8016-ln-dac-400`, `vertiv/acs8032-ln-dac-400`
  and `vertiv/acs8048-ln-dac-400`). Each is the dual-AC unit of the same front with two SMA antenna jacks, printed AUX
  and CELL, and the word CELLULAR under the product name; the rear is the rear of the unit with the same port count.
  The jacks are hidden behind the antennas in the one photograph held, so their positions are estimated from the
  antenna bases and the printed labels. The antennas, the SIM slot cover on the top and the curved line printed
  beside CELL are not drawn, and each device records why. No part is added and no existing part or device changes,
  and no row of the port records is closed.
