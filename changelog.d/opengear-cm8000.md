### Added
- Opengear CM8000 console managers: the CM8004, CM8004-C, CM8008 and CM8008-C
  as four devices with a front and a rear drawn from the maker's photographs
  and the data sheet envelope. Each has four or eight RJ45 serial ports, two
  combo network ports drawn as an SFP cage and an RJ45 jack under one number,
  two USB-A ports, a USB-C console port, an erase pinhole, six lamps and two
  12 VDC power jacks; the -C models add four SMA antenna jacks and two SIM
  slots. Every position read off a photograph is declared an estimate with its
  method, and no rack ear or kit is drawn because no document dimensions one.
  New parts `opengear/sim-slot-4ff@1` and `opengear/sim-tray-4ff@1`, with the
  interface `sim-tray-4ff` the tray seats in, and the maker `opengear`. This
  closes no rows of the port records. No existing device, part or port
  changes.
