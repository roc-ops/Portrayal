### Added
- **Enlogic EB0952** (`enlogic/eb0952`), **Enlogic EN2333** (`enlogic/en2333`) and **Enlogic EN6324** (`enlogic/en6324`). They are the basic
  three-phase PDU and the input-metered and outlet-metered single-phase Advantage Series PDUs, 0U, 1780 and 1750 mm long. Each
  has a fixed cord and its outlets. The cord ends in an IEC 60309 3P+E 9h plug on the EB0952 and a NEMA L6-30P plug on the
  other two. Each bank is behind its own 20 A double-pole breaker. The two Advantage units draw the hot-swap network module. All six faces are stated; the two
  toolless buttons on the back are placed 1555 mm apart as the maker's drawings dimension them. The devices close no row of
  the records file.
- **`enlogic/pdu-cord-l6-30p@1`**: the fixed input cord of the single-phase units, a `nema-l6-30p` power port. The gland's size
  is read from the maker's drawings and its end-on shape is an estimate, declared in the contract.
