### Added
- Juniper EX3300 access switches, from the EX3300 Switch Hardware Guide (published 2020-12-15): the
  `juniper/ex3300-48t` (configurations `ac`, EX3300-48T, and `bf`, the back-to-front EX3300-48T-BF),
  `juniper/ex3300-48p`, `juniper/ex3300-24t` (configurations `ac`, EX3300-24T, and `dc`, the DC-powered
  EX3300-24T-DC) and `juniper/ex3300-24p`. The power supply and the fan are built into the chassis, so
  none of them has a bay.
- Four Juniper EX3300 parts: `juniper/ex3300-lcd-button@1` (Menu and Enter), `juniper/ex3300-dc-terminal@1`
  (the four-terminal DC block), `juniper/ex3300-rps-port@1` and `juniper/ex3300-rear-grille@1`. The
  earthing terminal is `juniper/ex3400-ground-pair@1`, reused.
