### Changed
- The ports on the eight FHD duplex fibre adapter panels (the 12-adapter LC panels in OS2 UPC, OS2 APC
  and OM3/OM4, the 18-adapter LC panels in OS2 and OM3/OM4, and the 6-adapter SC panels in OS2 UPC,
  OS2 APC and OM3/OM4), front and rear contract each, now join a port group, so an adapter on a
  panel seated in an enclosure says what it is for. Each face has one group, `adapters`, with the role
  `traffic` and the media `fiber`. The FS datasheet prints the fibre and the polish of these panels and
  no data rate, so no group states a speed. Part ids, positions and drawn geometry are unchanged; each
  contract takes a minor and the seven FHD enclosures that can seat them a patch.
