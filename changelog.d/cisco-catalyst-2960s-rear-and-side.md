### Changed
- The five Cisco Catalyst 2960-S (`cisco/ws-c2960s-48fpd`, `-48lpd`, `-48td`, `-24pd` and `-24td`, Refs #1275) draw more of
  the rear that the NetBox photograph of the WS-C2960S-48LPD-L shows. That is the lit top edge, the light rim round the AC
  inlet surround, the two oval tabs above and below the inlet and the small round hole left of the fan. It is also the twelve
  stamped bracket marks in two groups of six, the chassis' diagonal edge at the far right and the steps under it. Every
  figure is read at 3.41 px per mm and is estimated. No port, lamp or group id moves.
- The same five devices draw the left wall's 29 openings (17 slots, 7 round holes and 5 small rectangles) read off Figure 1-10
  of the Catalyst 2960-S Switch Hardware Installation Guide. The figure shows them; the guide does not say what they are for.
  They stay drawn as marks in a stand-in dark, and the wall keeps the device's colour.
- The two 299 mm models assume that the front group of openings keeps its distance from the front edge. Figure 1-10 names no
  model, so that is an assumption and not a reading.
- The printed legends on the rear (the RATING label, the line above the RPS plate) and the maker's logo are left to a later
  touch of their own. The AC inlet's pin pockets, the slots' waists and the right wall are each a later part of their own.
- The RPS connector part (`cisco/rps2300-connector@2`, seated by the same five devices) draws the notch in the left edge of its
  plate, 2.6 mm deep, read off the same photograph. The five devices take a patch bump each.
- Two older gap rows of each device, `rear-undrawn-details` and `the-four-undrawn-faces`, are replaced with narrower ones.
