### Changed
- The five Cisco Catalyst 2960-S (`cisco/ws-c2960s-48fpd`, `-48lpd`, `-48td`, `-24pd` and `-24td`, Refs #1275) draw more of
  their rear. The NetBox photograph of the WS-C2960S-48LPD-L shows it. That is the lit top edge, the light rim round the AC
  inlet surround and the two oval tabs above and below the inlet. It is also the small round hole left of the fan and the
  twelve stamped bracket marks in two groups of six. The chassis' diagonal edge at the far right and the steps under it are
  drawn too. Every figure is read at 3.41 px per mm and is estimated. No port, lamp or group id moves.
- The same five devices draw the left wall's 29 openings (17 slots, 7 round holes and 5 small rectangles). They are placed
  from two photographs of the product, a 24-port and a 48-port model, both 299 mm deep. Figure 1-10 of the Catalyst 2960-S
  Switch Hardware Installation Guide gives the pattern. The photographs give the positions and sizes, good to about 4 mm along
  the wall and 2 mm across. The top row of slots stands about 15 mm apart, as the photographs show, where a flat reading of
  the figure gives 21 mm. The figure shows the openings; the guide does not say what they are for. They stay drawn as marks
  in a stand-in dark, and the wall keeps the device's colour.
- The 24-port photograph shows the front group of the 24TD. The 48TD applies the 24TD's pattern, as it has the same length.
  The three 386 mm models assume that each group keeps its distance from its own end, which leaves a plain stretch 87 mm longer.
- The printed legends on the rear (the RATING label and the line above the RPS plate) are left to a later touch. The maker's
  logo is left too. The AC inlet's pin pockets, the slots' waists and the right wall are each a later part of their own.
- The RPS connector part (`cisco/rps2300-connector@2`) draws a notch in the left edge of its plate, 2.6 mm deep. It is read
  off the same photograph and seated by the same five devices. The five devices take a patch bump each.
- Two older gap rows of each device, `rear-undrawn-details` and `the-four-undrawn-faces`, are replaced with narrower ones.
