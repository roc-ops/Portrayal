### Added
- `lantronix/lm4`, the Lantronix LM4 compact console server: a 44.45 by 158.75 by 158.75 mm body with six faces. The front
  shows four RJ45 serial ports, one RJ45 Ethernet port, a console jack, an SFP cage and two USB-A ports. The brief's two
  models, the 4-port LM4-4S and the 2-port LM4-2S, are listed as two builds. Both are drawn with four jacks, which for
  the LM4-2S is a drawing choice and not the maker's count of two; its export says so. Positions on the front are read
  from the maker's one angled photograph, to about 1 mm where a mask read them and by eye elsewhere, so the front is
  declared estimated. The DC input connector, the rear, the small marks and icons, the lit lamps, the lid slats, the
  flank screws and the rack ear are left as named gaps.
