### Added
- `lantronix/lm4`, the Lantronix LM4 compact console server: a 44.45 by 158.75 by 158.75 mm body with six faces. The front
  shows four RJ45 serial ports, one RJ45 Ethernet port, a console jack, an SFP cage and two USB-A ports. The brief's two
  models, the 4-port LM4-4S and the 2-port LM4-2S, are listed as two builds. Both are drawn with four jacks, which for
  the LM4-2S is a drawing choice and not the maker's count of two; its export says so. Positions on the front are read
  from the maker's one angled photograph. They hold to about 1 mm where a mask read them and are by eye elsewhere, so
  the front is declared estimated. The small marks and icons are drawn as silkscreen paths. The lid shows its slats and the left
  flank its three screws, estimated from views at a grazing angle. The photograph shows the lamps of the console and
  serial jacks lit. The lamps are drawn in their off state by default. Their on states carry the photograph's colours,
  which a viewer sets. The DC input connector, the
  registered mark, the rear and the rack ear are left as named gaps.
