### Added
- `lantronix/lm4`, the Lantronix LM4 compact console server: a 44.45 by 158.75 by 158.75 mm body with six faces. The front
  shows four RJ45 serial ports, one RJ45 Ethernet port, a console jack, an SFP cage and two USB-A ports. It has no
  expansion bay. The brief's two models, the 4-port LM4-4S and the 2-port LM4-2S, are listed as two builds drawn alike, because no held document says how many jacks the 2-port unit has. Every
  position on the front is read from the maker's one angled photograph and is good to about 2 mm, so the front is
  declared estimated. The DC input connector, the rear, the lamps and the rack ear are left as gaps.
