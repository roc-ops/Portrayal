### Added
- Cisco Nexus 9500 chassis, batch 1: the Nexus 9508 (`cisco/n9k-c9508`, N9K-C9508, 13 RU) with its eight
  line-card slots (each holding its blank until the line cards are added), two supervisor slots, eight
  power-supply slots on the front, and three fan-tray, six fabric-module and two system-controller slots
  on the rear. The line cards, the rack kit, the Nexus 9504 and the Nexus 9516 follow.
- Cisco Nexus 9500 power supplies `cisco/n9k-pac-3000w-b`, `cisco/n9k-puv-3000w-b`,
  `cisco/n9k-puv2-3000w-b` and `cisco/n9k-pdc-3000w-b`; supervisors `cisco/n9k-sup-a`,
  `cisco/n9k-sup-a-plus`, `cisco/n9k-sup-b` and `cisco/n9k-sup-b-plus`; the system controller
  `cisco/n9k-sc-a`. The 9504 and 9516 take the same parts.
- Cisco Nexus 9508 fan trays `cisco/n9k-c9508-fan` and `cisco/n9k-c9508-fan2`; fabric modules
  `cisco/n9k-c9508-fm`, `-fm-s`, `-fm-e`, `-fm-e2`, `-fm-g`, `-fm-r` and `-fm-r2`; and the blanks
  `cisco/n9k-c9500-lc-cv` (line card), `cisco/n9k-c9508-fm-z`, `cisco/n9k-c9508-fm-cv`,
  `cisco/n9k-c9508-fan-pwr` (fabric slot) and `cisco/n9k-psu-blank` (power-supply slot).
