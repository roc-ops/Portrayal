### Added
- **Enlogic EB0803** (`enlogic/eb0803`): the basic three-phase PDU, 0U, 1490 mm long,
  with a fixed 3 m cord ending in a NEMA L21-30P plug and 30 unswitched outlets in three
  colour-coded banks of ten (eight IEC C13, one IEC C19 and one NEMA 5-20R each), every
  bank behind its own 20 A double-pole breaker. All six faces are stated; the two
  toolless buttons on the back are placed 1244 mm apart as the maker's drawing
  dimensions them, and the bank colours are read off the data sheet's face render. The
  device closes no row of the records file. The maker is registered as `enlogic`.
- **`enlogic/eb-breaker-20a-2p@1`**, **`enlogic/pdu-cord-l21-30p@1`** and
  **`enlogic/pdu-mounting-button@1`**: the bank breaker, whose plate colour and legends
  are fields; the fixed input cord, which exports as a NEMA L21-30P power port; and the
  toolless button, which mates `pdu-button`. The cord's end-on shape and the button's
  relief are estimates, declared in each contract.
