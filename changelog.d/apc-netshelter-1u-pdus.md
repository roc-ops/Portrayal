### Added
- Two APC by Schneider Electric NetShelter 1U rack PDUs, each with eight IEC C13 outlets and a C20 inlet, drawn on all six
  faces from the maker's product datasheets: the AP7821B (metered) and the AP7921B (switched). Every position on the outlet
  face is read off a small datasheet photograph and is declared an estimate in the device. Two new parts of the maker:
  `apc/ap78-display@1` (the two-digit display) and `apc/ap78-button@1` (the round button). Each device and each part is new,
  so nothing that exists changes; four rows of DCIM device types are new (NetBox and Nautobot, one per device).
