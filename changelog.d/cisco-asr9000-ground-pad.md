### Changed
- `cisco/a9k-ground-pad@1` (1.0.0 to 1.1.0), the NEBS bonding and grounding pad of the Cisco ASR 9006, 9010, 9901, 9902, 9903, 9904, 9906,
  9910, 9912 and 9922, now composes two 10-32 screws as parts and presents `stud-pair-5-8` at their midpoint, so a two-hole lug lands across
  the pair and a ring lug lands on either screw. The guides print the lug's hole spacing as a range, 0.625 to 0.75 in., and no figure
  dimensions the receptacle; the part draws the 5/8 in. end, which is the registered pitch, and its contract says the pitch is declared and
  what would settle it. The screws are the 10-32 screws the guides name, 9.47 mm across, where the drawing had two 3.6 mm heads, and they
  stand 15.875 mm apart where it had 15.3 mm. The pad's box, its lug and its earth mark are drawn as before.
- The ten chassis that place it each take a patch, with their locks and the drawing version line of their exported device types. No
  port, interface or id of a chassis moves. One row of the records file is gone.
