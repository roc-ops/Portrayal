### Changed
- The GPU power headers, cable plugs and NVLink parts have a 3D form instead of a
  box with the skin painted on one face (docs/connectors-gpu-design.md section 8).
  A header's cells are each their own recess, with a contact standing in each, the
  16-pin headers' four sense cells smaller, and a latch ramp on the housing. A plug
  is a tube with its cells open at the mating face, a rear wall, a latch arm on
  top, a short wire at each cell, a comb and the same 30 mm stub as before. The
  NVLink connector is a slot with a rim and two contact rows. The two-slot bridge
  is stepped: plate, two connector ends one two-slot pitch apart, a cover and a
  raised centre. It uses the library's existing relief keys and adds no new one;
  the heights are estimates and each says so. No size, interface or connection
  point changes.
