### Added
- **`nokia/nfxs-f-bb`**: the two brackets that hold the 7360 ISAM FX-4 shelf stood on its
  side, stated as `chassis.ears.rolled`, so a shelf rolled a quarter turn in a rack elevation
  draws a bar against each edge and an ear on each post instead of the generic ears. The bar,
  ear height and span are the sizes the vertical shelf mount kit's unit data sheet prints for
  each bracket; the ear width is an estimate, and the colour is the library's neutral silver.
  A gap names what no page draws. The power lug size, the cable exit and the open channel
  facing the shelf stay unmodelled. No record of the conformance list is closed.
