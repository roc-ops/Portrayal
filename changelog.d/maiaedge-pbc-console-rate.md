### Changed
- The MaiaEdge PBC-2000 (`maiaedge/pbc-2000`, 8.0.2 to 8.0.3) records its console rate, 115.2 kbps, in the `management` section of its
  attributes, from the data sheet's console-port row ("RJ45 115.2 kbps"). The data sheet's own wording is kept and no frame format is
  added, because the data sheet states none. Only the record and the drawing version line of its two exported device types move; no
  port, interface, id or pixel changes.
