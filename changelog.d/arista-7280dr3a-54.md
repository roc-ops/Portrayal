### Added
- The Arista 7280DR3A-54 (`arista/7280dr3a-54`, #1204): a 2RU switch with 54 400G QSFP-DD ports standing upright in eighteen
  columns of three and two OSFP line-system ports, its front and rear panels. Its DR3A, DR3AM and DR3AK builds are orderable
  configurations (DCS-7280DR3A-54-F, DCS-7280DR3AM-54-F and DCS-7280DR3AK-54-F), and a DC build is shown as a worked example.
- The 7280DR3A-54's 3000 W supplies PWR-3001-AC-RED and PWR-3001-DC-RED (`arista/7280dr3a-pwr-3000-ac`,
  `arista/7280dr3a-pwr-3000-dc`); its fan module is the 7280DR3A-36's FAN-7012H-RED (`arista/7280dr3a-fan`).
- The DCIM device types for the three 7280DR3A-54 builds and the module types for the two supplies, in the NetBox and Nautobot
  exports.
