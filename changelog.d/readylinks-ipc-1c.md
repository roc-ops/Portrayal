### Added
- **`readylinks/ipc-1c`**: the one-port ReadyLink coax client for the room end of a coax
  run, drawn from the maker's data sheet and 3D model. Its F connector is the `std/f-type@1`
  that the GL-12xC line card places, so a coax run lands at both ends; its 2.5G RJ45 port
  takes a cable; power comes in on a USB Type-C receptacle. Both long sides carry the seam
  between cover and base and the underside its four rubber feet. It closes no row of the port
  records.
