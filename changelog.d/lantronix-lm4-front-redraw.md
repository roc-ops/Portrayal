### Changed
- `lantronix/lm4` is redrawn from the maker's straight front and top views, which carry printed dimensions. The front is 159.0
  by 43.2 mm and the depth is 155.5 mm. The chassis takes those figures. The product brief prints 158.75 by 44.45 by
  158.75 mm, and both are in the device's notes. Every front figure is re-measured. That covers the heatsink and its six
  fins, the corner screws, the button, the Reset pinhole and E1. It also covers the SFP cage, the console and USB housing,
  the four serial jacks, the lamps, the bars and the marks. The lid's heatsink field follows the top view. The device takes a
  major version, because ports and parts moved.
- The three front lamps now carry the documented states. Power is solid green. Status and Warning carry the flash patterns of
  the Local Manager documentation. All are drawn off by default. The two small lamps at the lower corners of the SFP cage were
  drawn as dark dots. They are lamps, with the colours the maker's figure shows. The Reset pinhole is named for what it is.
- The housings follow the outer edge of their outline in the maker's front view. That covers the console and USB housing, the
  serial housing, the DC housing, and a housing behind E1 and behind the SFP cage. Each moved by up to 1.0 mm.
- The rack ear the documentation shows is not drawn. The library states ears for a rack device only, and the LM4 is a desktop
  unit. It stays a named gap. The DC input housing is drawn as decor with its four cells. Its latch tab has a pale window,
  a notch and a pale bar along its top. Its connector stays a named gap.
  The DIN rail fitting is a named gap. The right flank, which no document shows, is built as the left flank mirrored. The Reset
  pinhole has its tan ring, and the SFP cage has its four rim marks. The rear stays a named gap, because no held document
  shows it. This closes no row.
