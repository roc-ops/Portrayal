### Added
- The Arista 7280CR3A-24D12 (`arista/7280cr3a-24d12`, #1204): a 1RU switch with 24 100G QSFP ports and 12 400G QSFP-DD ports,
  its front and rear panels. Its CR3A, CR3AM, CR3AK and CR3AK-S builds are orderable configurations in front-to-rear and
  rear-to-front air (DCS-7280CR3A-24D12-F and -R, DCS-7280CR3AM-24D12-F and -R, DCS-7280CR3AK-24D12-F and -R,
  DCS-7280CR3AK-24D12-S-F and -S-R), and DC builds are shown as worked examples.
- The Arista 7280CR3A-48D6 (`arista/7280cr3a-48d6`): a 2RU switch with 48 100G QSFP ports, 6 400G QSFP-DD ports and one OSFP
  line-system port. Its CR3A, CR3AM and CR3AK builds are orderable configurations in both airflows (DCS-7280CR3A-48D6-F and -R,
  DCS-7280CR3AM-48D6-F and -R, DCS-7280CR3AK-48D6-F and -R).
- The Arista 7280CR3A-72 (`arista/7280cr3a-72`): a 2RU switch with 72 100G QSFP ports in nine blocks. Its CR3A, CR3AM and
  CR3AK builds are orderable configurations in both airflows (DCS-7280CR3A-72-F and -R, DCS-7280CR3AM-72-F and -R,
  DCS-7280CR3AK-72-F and -R).
- The three CR3A fronts are drawn from the 7280R3A data sheet's photographs, because the quick start guide has no front figure for
  them; every front position is estimated and says so.
- The 7280CR3A supplies PWR-1511-AC-BLUE (`arista/7280cr3a-pwr-1511-ac-rev`) and PWR-1513-AC-RED and -BLUE
  (`arista/7280cr3a-pwr-1513-ac`, `arista/7280cr3a-pwr-1513-ac-rev`); the other supplies and the fan modules are the R3, R3A and
  7050X4 parts already in the library.
- The DCIM device types for the 20 orderable builds and the module types for the new supplies, in the NetBox and Nautobot exports.
