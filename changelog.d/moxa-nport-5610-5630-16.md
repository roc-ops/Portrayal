### Added
- The Moxa NPort 5610-16 (RS-232) and NPort 5630-16 (RS-422/485) rackmount serial device servers as two devices
  (`moxa/nport-5610-16`, `moxa/nport-5630-16`), each with all six faces. They have the body, the front, the AC rear and the top of
  the NPort 5650-16: the LCD panel and four push buttons, the two rows of 16 Tx and Rx lamps, the 16 RJ45 serial ports in two
  groups of eight, the LAN jack, the AC inlet with its rocker switch and the ground screw. They differ from it in the serial
  standard, the weight and the input current the data sheet lists for each model. The left and right sides, the underside and the
  rack ears are drawn from the maker's 2D CAD sheet, which is titled for all four 16-port models. It prints no hole
  position for them, so each is read off the drawing at its scale and declared an estimate. No part is added: both place only parts
  the NPort 5650-16 already places. The sides and underside of the NPort 5650-16 and 5650-16-T stay as they are. This closes no
  rows of the port records.
