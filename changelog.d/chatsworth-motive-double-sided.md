  as `-703` (black) and `-E03` (Glacier White), 7 ft tall, with black fingers. Each is a `rack-side` part that stands
  beside the rack and takes no rack unit, with two faces back to back: a T-shaped cable guide to each rack unit down

  overall size and marked estimated, as are the guides, the depth of each channel, the frame and the doors. The
  accessories and the cable capacity (the maker's cable fill table is not held) are stated as gaps. Twenty-one new
  components; one guide serves every part and no existing device or component changes (#1093).
