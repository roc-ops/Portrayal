### Added
- The Vertiv Geist VP7553 and VP7557, two basic vertical rack PDUs, each a 1683 mm strip 51 mm wide and deep.
  The VP7553 has 20 C13 and 4 C19 outlets in two circuits, each behind a 16 A magnetic breaker.
  The VP7557 has 36 C13 and 6 C19 outlets over three phases and no breaker.
  Outlets are drawn on the front, placed from the maker's data sheet figure and photograph at about 2 mm per pixel.
  Each has a fixed 3 m input cord that exports as a power port of type other, labelled with its IEC 60309 plug.
  Three new parts, two cords and the breaker. The back, the ends and the sides stay named gaps, as no held image shows them.
