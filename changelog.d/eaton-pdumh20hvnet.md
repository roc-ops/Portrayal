### Added
- The Eaton Tripp Lite series switched PDU PDUMH20HVNET, a 1U horizontal unit for 208/230 V, 16 A. It has eight
  individually switched IEC C13 outlets, each with a lamp. It also has a pre-installed WEBCARDLX network card, a load
  meter and a C20 inlet for a detachable cord. Its size follows the proportion of the maker's rear photograph. The rear
  is read off that photograph. The front is photographed at an angle, so it is placed by a fitted map and marked
  estimated. The ears are separate brackets and are not drawn. Every face is drawn or says why it is not. The DE-9 factory port is drawn
  male, as on the PDUMH20NET, and carries a declared gap. The change closes no rows of the port-usable records.
