### Added
- `lantronix/lm83x`, the Lantronix LM83X console server: a 1U body of 445 by 43.2 by 264 mm with six faces. The base unit
  shows eight RJ45 serial ports, two Ethernet jacks, a console jack, an SFP cage, a USB-A and a USB-C port and two SMA
  connectors. Three expansion bays take the three modules below, and the rear shows two AC inlets and the vents. The
  default build leaves the bays empty, and two example builds seat the modules as the maker's illustration and
  photographs show them. Positions are read off the maker's front illustration. Lamp states, the option slot, the
  16-port and 32-port modules, the DC model and the ear positions are left as gaps.
- `lantronix/lm80`, the Lantronix LM80 compact console server: a 254 by 43 by 163 mm body with the same base ports and
  no expansion bay. It is stated as a desktop unit because no held document shows a rack mount. The rear is stated
  empty because no held document shows it.
- `lantronix/lm83x-8sa@1`, `lantronix/lm83x-8ea@1` and `lantronix/lm83x-lcda@1`, the 8-port serial, 8-port Ethernet and
  LCD modules of the LM83X, each 92.9 mm wide as the maker's module table states. No power draw is stated for any of
  them, so the lint findings for it are recorded in the baseline.
