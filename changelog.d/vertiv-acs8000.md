### Added
- The Vertiv Avocent ACS8000 advanced console server as four devices, the single-AC units with 8, 16, 32 and 48
  RJ45 serial ports (`vertiv/acs8008sac-400`, `acs8016sac-400`, `acs8032sac-400` and `acs8048sac-400`). Each draws
  both faces: the AC inlet and power switch, the digital-in and sensor jacks, six USB ports and the SD card slot on the
  front, and the serial ports, console jack, two LAN jacks, two SFP cages, two USB ports and three lamps on the rear.
  No drawing is held, so every position is measured off the maker's photographs and is declared estimated. The
  maker `vertiv` joins the vendor registry.
- Three parts: `vertiv/sd-card-slot@1` presents a new `sd-card` interface that `common/sd-card@1` mates, and
  `vertiv/acs8000-rocker@1` is the small power switch. Their sizes are declared estimated.
