### Changed
- The part-conformance check counts a seat the build publishes. A drawn screw, stud or inlet is a place to land a cord or lug
  (C20) when the build publishes a connector slot at its own path, of a registered interface, that takes a cable end; the poles
  of a DC port are such seats, which the free-port list shows as the port and not one by one. A block that a placed part draws
  with no part of its own is judged by the seats drawn inside it, and a terminal drawn with no published seat is still
  reported. Nothing in the library, its exports or any lock changes, and no row of the port-usable records file moves.
